## Options
- **C with the disc.** A volley fires its rounds as the weapon does, and unless a ring is live it also opens the disc D-070 built.
- **C as drafted** (draft 10): the rounds with the port's band of 100 uu. The same fight and the same modifiers; the band adds almost nothing (170 to 717 a minute on the boss).
- **S kept, for a TUNE on `dps`.** Declined before it was asked: that step was written to follow a pass.
- **Hold the card for people.**

## Decision
C with the disc, in nine items.

1. R-RFG-12 becomes: a Pulse Wall volley fires its rounds as the weapon does, with the weapon's count, damage, speed, reach and pierce and whatever its modifiers do; and unless this weapon has a ring live, the volley also opens one ring at the hero. The ring grows to `radius_uu` over `grow_s` and holds for `hold_s`; each `tick_s`, every enemy inside its current radius is struck for `dps` times one round's damage times `tick_s` and pushed radially outward; an enemy that ignores displacement is struck and stays. The weapon's cooldown is its own; one ring per weapon lives at a time.
2. The disc's cells are D-070's, the source's: no band, `dps` 6, `push_uu_s` 73 flat, once a tick.
3. The capabilities stay the port's (Projectile, Radial, Area, Expiry). All fourteen modifiers that fit the Ring Emitter stay live and act on the rounds. D-070's rulings 10 and 11 fall away with it: no flag cell changes, `weapons.md` R-WPN-08 takes no exception, and D-073 has nothing to hand back on this weapon.
4. What a ring's strike carries, stated so that nothing multiplies: the Keeper's reactions that act on it today (status and chain) and nothing a modifier places or moves. A modifier's placements (Split Core, Scatter Shell, Lingering Field) do not fire on a ring's strike, as today. Backwash does not pull on a ring's strike: the wall's push is that strike's one displacement, and a modifier that drags the crowd onto the hero four times a second would turn the wall inside out. The job's first step is the table of the fourteen under this rule, from code and the harness, with a test that binds the two sentences on placements and on Backwash.
5. Before any reading of play the job reads whether the weapon's rounds are throttled by the disc's strikes (a chain's jump spends an emission, and the disc strikes everything inside four times a second), from the weapon record's throttled count on a harness scene. If rounds are throttled, it stops and reports; it tunes nothing round it.
6. Why this is still an evolution and not the plain weapon with more: it holds ground. A ring that strikes and pushes whatever stands within 600 uu, for most of every ring life, is something the plain weapon never did, and it is what the card says. What the reading took away is the source's price for it, the rounds, and three readings say that price is the run. The price can come back as a number (item 8), not as a weapon that cannot hurt a boss.
7. This is the form for an evolution on this project until a reading says otherwise: it keeps its weapon's own emission and changes it or adds to it. An evolution that deletes the emission is not built again without a reading, by a bot that uses the weapon or by people, that shows it holds a run. Blade Halo is ruled on its own readings.
8. Output. With the rounds back and the disc at the source's strength the build may pass `REFORGE.POWER_MAX` before the boss. If it does, one TUNE pass on `dps` downward is a step of its own. If it lands under `REFORGE.POWER_MIN`, as every evolution does on this instrument, nothing is done for that alone.
9. C as drafted, with the port's band, is the fallback, built only if item 5 or the perf line stops this one.

## Hypothesis
We believe that letting a Pulse Wall volley keep its rounds and open the source's disc moves the build's damage per minute against its hold to at least 0.95 before the boss and at least 0.9 in the boss fight, each judged by itself, because the rounds are what struck the boss (5 793 a minute against the disc's 2 069) and what made Tempest's children, and the disc adds to them where it replaced them; measured by `Tools/BotRuns.ps1 -Carry` on the Ring Emitter pair `Steered:Storm:Tempest:Tide`, 15 seeds, bot v2 at skill 1.0, read by `Tools/evolve_read.py --check-windows 0.95 0.9`; printed beside them and deciding nothing: the Arc Grid pair, the port's arm on the same build's base and seeds, the nearest enemy, live enemies, damage taken and deaths before the boss, the fight's length, reach and wins; reverted, the rule put back to the port's, if the build reads under 0.95 of its hold before the boss or under 0.9 in the boss fight.

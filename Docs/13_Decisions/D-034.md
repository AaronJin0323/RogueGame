# D-034 — The S3 exit's "pass 2 of 3" is two of three validators on each of the two Vision rows written that way

- **Date:** 2026-11-09
- **Decider:** scheduler (delegated, D-009); a go/no-go rule, so it stays the owner's to reverse in the window (`Harness/H08 §2`)
- **Class:** GO-NOGO
- **Status:** replaced by D-049 (2026-11-11): the S3 exit passes on the designer's two of three, and fresh means a fresh save

## Context
`12_Production §2` gives S3's exit as "3 fresh validators × 3 runs, pass 2 of 3" and says nothing more. `Harness/H03` and `H07` give "e.g. 2 of 3 criteria" and list none, and `H07 §4` wants the pass rule written before the session. `dev/s3-kit` named it call 1 for a D-number (`Validation/findings_S3.md`, Calls) and left the `Pass rule` row NOT READ whatever is filled. `00_Vision §4` has four success criteria: two are written as ≥ 2 of 3 testers (another run asked for unprompted; the archetype named in their own words), one is a band read by tools, one is about the owner.

## Options
- A — two of three criteria, any two of the four: lets the two tester rows both miss and still pass on a band and the owner, which is not a test of three people.
- B — two of three validators on each of the two Vision rows written as 2 of 3, both required.
- C — two of three validators overall, each judged on every row: invents a per-person verdict no doc holds.

## Decision
B. The S3 exit passes its rule when at least 2 of V1, V2 and V3 read Y on "asked for another run unprompted" and at least 2 read Y on "named their archetype in their own words", both from `findings_S3.md`, as the host records them. The 3 are the validators the `Validators` row accepts, so `pass 2 of 3` is read only once that row passes. The core-feel bands and the owner's row stay read and reported in `findings_S3.md`; they are not part of this rule. The rule is read by a person from the filled file, never from the logs, so `Tools/S3Exit.ps1` keeps the row NOT READ and names this record. Calls 2 to 6 and 8 stay open: the tools keep their strict sides.

## Hypothesis
We believe a written pass rule moves the S3 exit from a row that can never be read to one a host fills from two Y/N answers per validator, because the docs wrote only those two criteria as 2 of 3; measured at the external S3 session; reverted if the owner reads a session that passes it as one that did not show the game working, or the reverse.

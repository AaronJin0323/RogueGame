## Decision
The scheduler decides open questions itself instead of waiting on the owner window: each decision is still a `D-nnn.md` (Decider: "scheduler (delegated, D-009)") with its evidence and hypothesis, so the owner can review and reverse any of them. Still the owner's: anything only a human can do (validation sessions with non-owner players, sign-ins, installs on the Mac), and anything outward-facing beyond the repo or irreversible (publishing elsewhere, spending money, deleting remote data).

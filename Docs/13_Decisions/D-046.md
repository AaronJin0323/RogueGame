# D-046 — A chest restores health; the two pressure bands are set at the S1 gate

- **Date:** 2026-11-11
- **Decider:** scheduler (delegated, D-009; the owner on 2026-11-11: "make the best decision for the game and fix docs, then tell development agents")
- **Class:** RULE (the restore) and SHAPE (how the two bands get their values)
- **Status:** open (decided; the restore is applied on `main` by the change job that cites it; the bands are set by the gate decision after session A)

## Context
`Validation/plans_audit.md` 3.2 and 5.1.

- `C.DMG_TAKEN.BAND_MIN10_13` asks for 30 to 50 % of max HP lost per minute over 180 s: 90 to 150 % of a bar. The hero has `C.HERO.MAX_HP` 100 and nothing restores it. `director.md` Open and `Validation/S1_balance_pass2.md` §3 item 8 both say the band and reaching the boss cannot both hold. The S1 exit still required it.
- The number is the previous game's. Its table reads ≤ 10 % at minute 2, 15–30 % at minute 10 and 30–50 % at minute 18 of a twenty-minute run that had Lifeline and regeneration (`Handoff_Dusktracer/game/reference/original_docs/01_GameDesign.md` §3.5). The blueprint carried the last row to minutes ten to thirteen.
- No decision removed recovery. Lifeline went out with the multiplier modifiers (`modifiers.md`), regeneration went out with meta stats (pillar 5), and the band stayed.
- The source's rhythm needs a valve: Threat is where "damage taken is *expected*", and Breath, where the chest drops, is "the anti-fatigue valve — every spike has a visible end".
- The one human run: ten hits, HP never rose, down at 563 s with three chests collected (`Validation/S1_owner_run.md`). Bots at skill 1.0 lose about 7 % of a bar in a whole run.
- A run with no recovery is decided by its first eight mistakes whatever was built, which sets pillar 1 against pillar 2.

## Options
- A — No recovery. Re-derive the band as a hit budget.
- B — A chest restores a share of max HP to the hero who collects it. Six chests a run already exist, one per Threat beat (R-DIR-16); the heal call already exists and is tested (`Reserve.Restore`). The bands are then set from people's readings.
- C — A Restore card always offered in a chest hand. A real choice, but it rewrites hand rules (non-negotiable 5) before anyone has played a hand.
- D — Change nothing until session A. The first validators then play a game its own rhythm says is missing a part, and few of their runs reach the minutes the exit reads.

## Decision
B, and no band is invented.

**The restore.** `progression.md` R-PRG-09 gains: collecting a chest also restores `C.PICKUP.CHEST_RESTORE_HP_RATIO` of max HP to the collector through `UDTHeroSubsystem::Heal`, never above max, and a hero who is down stays down. New constant, GATED(S1), first value 0.15, `[G]`: one chest forgives about one mid-run Tank contact (14.8 to 19.0 in the owner's run). Six chests return at most 0.9 of a bar across a run. Telemetry gains a `hero_healed{owner amount source}` record, which `11_Quality §3` has promised since S0.

RULE, not SYSTEM: one clause on an existing pickup, an existing call, one constant; no verb, screen, string or subsystem. It replaces an unwritten rule, that nothing restores HP in a run.

**The bands.** `C.DMG_TAKEN.BAND_MIN10_13` and `C.DASHES.BAND_MIN10_13` keep their inherited values as drafts and are judged at no session until they are set. Session A reads every minute of every run (D-048). One gate decision then sets both from those readings by the method in `10_Tuning.md §2`, inside two bounds that are arithmetic: a run in the band through the whole window must still be able to reach the boss, given max HP and the restores it can collect; and the lower edge is at least one median hit per minute. Session B confirms, and then `08_Constants §1` locks.

Until then no pressure number (timeline, slopes, caps, drop-in cadence) is tuned against either band.

## Hypothesis
We believe a chest restore of 0.15 moves the share of validator runs alive at 600 s from an unknown that the owner's one run puts at none to at least half of the six, and leaves pressure felt, because the owner lost a whole bar in ten hits with nothing coming back; measured at session A from `run_end`, `hero_healed` and kit question 4; reverted (the clause and the constant removed) if at least two of three validators reach the boss above half HP on their second run and name nothing that threatened them.

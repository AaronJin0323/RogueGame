# D-016 — Pull holds each enemy for an interval; fields apply their status

- **Date:** 2026-10-30
- **Decider:** scheduler (delegated, D-009)
- **Class:** RULE
- **Status:** applied

## Context
1. `Whirlpool.Key` Pull fires on every impact and moves every enemy within `radius_uu` by `distance_uu` toward the impact, a point next to the hero, 4 or more times a second. It works as an enemy speed boost. Primary Whirlpool dies at 110 s (`S1_balance_pass2.md` §3 item 5), and in second-Tide arms runs with the Key reached the boss 1 of 77 against 7 of 13 without it (`S1_tide_expression.md` §3; few and offer-selected, an indication). Lowering `distance_uu` did not answer it (§6 round 1).
2. A field's `status` had no reader, so Furnace's and Downpour's fields applied nothing (`S1_tide_expression.md` §6 item 1).
3. Whirlpool's layer `Flag.Pull{on=Impact;distance_uu=150}` named no radius. Its `on` word made it a separate entry, which pulled nothing (§6 item 2).

## Decision
1. **R-KEE-21:** a `Flag.Pull` moves a given enemy at most once per `C.KEEPER.PULL_INTERVAL_S`, per enemy, stored with the enemy fields and counted in sim steps. Direction and `distance_uu` do not change. What rides on a pull rides on the applied pull only.
2. The rule covers every `Flag.Pull`: the same flag gets one rule, and `Mod.Backwash` pulls toward the round's origin, the same exposure. The mine's pull (speed-limited, its own constants) and the Tide Intercession's line are other mechanics and are not held.
3. **R-KEE-22:** a field applies its `status` every `C.STATUS.BURN_TICK_S`, once per enemy per source. Not every step: a Burn past its cap replaces its oldest stack and restarts that stack's tick, so step-rate or overlapping applications would replace stacks before they ever dealt damage. No new number.
4. Whirlpool's layer loses `on=Impact` and folds into the Key's entry, as a plain value replacing `distance_uu` for the cast. A separate entry with a radius would be held by rule 1 behind the Key's pull on the same impact and still do nothing. The layer implies the Key (R-KEE-06), so an entry with its radius always exists. No number changes.

## Hypothesis
Recorded in `number_change_log.csv` before measuring: primary Whirlpool median end > 300 s and reaches L8 in most runs; second-Tide arms reach the boss ≥ 5/15. At most two interval values are tried; if neither meets it, rule 1 is reverted and this record says so.

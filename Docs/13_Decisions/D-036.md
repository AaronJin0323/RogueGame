What the numbers say for play:
- The farthest point an enemy attack can start, in any direction, is 1300 uu: the Warden's hazard ring at its minimum (`C.BOSS.HAZARD_MIN_RADIUS_UU`), just past a Turret at `C.ROLE.TURRET_FIRE_RANGE_UU` with its own radius, and past the threat ring's outer edge (`C.DIR.THREAT_RING_MAX_UU`) where drop-in markers land. A Rusher starts its charge within `C.ROLE.RUSHER_TRIGGER_UU`. Add one Tank diameter so a source is a body on screen and not a sliver at the edge: 1416 uu. At pitch 60 the down-screen reach is about R / 100 m, so the frame radius must be at least about 1412. R 1400 misses by 0.2 m; the book misses by 6.6 m, and half of all Turret positions in range, and half the hazard ring, would be off screen.
- Fire that hurts is never surprise fire if its source is on screen: a Turret's round still takes 1.9 s from its range and follows a 0.75 s tell (R-ENM-12, R-ENM-13) whatever the frame. The bottom-edge times only matter for what is off screen, so the reach clause is the fairness line, not the reaction times.
- The player is the bullet hell (pillar 1): every S1 weapon's base range, the Ring Emitter's 1350 uu the longest, stays in view at R 1500. The book frame shows a third of a Ring Emitter's circle at its end and cuts the Rail Lance off at 9 %.
- Over the floor, a tighter frame buys pixels. At 1500 the Orbital Drone round reaches 4.3 px in the 720p gate frame (3.2 today), the swarm hull 23 px at 1080p (17 today), and every art detail gets about 1.78 times the pixel area of today.
- The 400 enemy cap fits on screen with room at every option. Spawns cannot be off screen in every direction with any frame that keeps attacks on screen: the frame is 16:9 and the ring is a circle, so the sides always reach past it. At 1500, 94 % of regular spawns appear in view (100 % today).
- Perf (D-011): today's frame shows everything at caps, so today's readings are a ceiling for instance count. Each object covers more pixels, so additive overdraw grows; the perf scenes are rerun after the move.

## Options
- A — Keep 2000. Safe on the fairness line with 7 m spare, but rounds of 3 to 5 px, a 17 px swarm hull and a 24 px hero are what make the S4 detail budgets unworkable, and the frame shows twice the ground play needs.
- B — The book's frame (pitch 62, 29 m back). Best for art, wrong for play: half the Turret range, half the hazard ring and most drop-in markers off screen, the player's own fire cut off at its end, spawns 86 % off screen. It breaks R-ENM-13's intent that every attack is seen before it lands.
- C — The tightest frame that keeps every attack start on screen with a body's margin: R 1500 at pitch 60 and FOV 50.

## Decision
C. The solo camera frames `C.ARENA.CAMERA_FRAME_RADIUS_UU` = 1500 uu of half-height at pitch 60 and horizontal FOV 50, about 53 m across at 36 px/m on 1080p. 1500 is the round value over the 1412 floor; the extra 0.8 m of reach keeps a source clear of the hero block in the bottom-left corner.

1. **Rule.** `06_UX.md` is written with its camera section: R-UX-01 (the frame's down-screen reach covers the farthest attack start plus a Tank's diameter) and R-UX-02 (the frame is fixed during a solo run). Both tests are PLANNED until the implementation lands.
2. **Fixed, not dynamic.** No zoom with threat, density or the boss. A moving frame changes every pixel size the contrast tool and the telegraph edge are tuned for, exactly when reading matters most, and moves the distance a player has learned to read. The Warden fits: at its standoff (2.6 hull radii) its far edge is 12.2 m from the hero, inside the 15.0 m reach, at 245 px across. The hazard ring at its minimum is fully in view.
3. **Not a player setting.** A closer zoom breaks R-UX-01; a wider one shrinks rounds back toward today's 3 px gate figure, and every human reading and telemetry comparison would be taken at an unknown frame. Text size is the accessibility lever (`dev/s2` `settings.md`). Reopened only on playtest evidence, and then bounded between this frame and 2000, the two frames with readings.
4. **Co-op is the one expected variation.** A shared camera (`coop.md`, D-004, not written) may widen the frame to hold both heroes, never narrower than R-UX-01. That widening is its own decision, and `Tools/Perf.ps1` then measures its widest frame.
5. **Pitch and FOV stay** at 60 and 50. The book's 62 is an art call with no play effect worth a change: a telegraph circle squashes to 0.866 of its width at 60 and 0.883 at 62.
6. **The sim does not move.** `C.ARENA.CAMERA_FRAME_RADIUS_UU` has sim readers: Echo's whole-screen strike (`Combat/DTWeaponSim.cpp`) and the Storm net (`Keepers/DTKeeperSim.cpp`) on `main`, R-KEE-29 and R-KEE-35 on `dev/s3`, and `keepers.md` calls it the screen-wide scope (`C.INTERCESSION.TIDE_RADIUS_UU` equals it). Those readers move to a new sim key at 2000 before the camera key moves, so no damage, hash or bot result changes.

Held, not decided here (no gameplay number is tuned by this record):
- Whether "screen-wide" sim effects should follow the new frame. At 2000 they reach past the frame's bottom edge and stop short of its sides. A RULE or TUNE call with its own evidence.
- Spawn pop-in: 94 % of regular spawns appear in view. Either a presentation arrival cue or a director ring change measured on bots; neither is this record.
- The Warden is the one attack source with no range bound. The hazard ring keeps it near, but it can be off screen in phases II and III; whether it needs an edge marker is read at the first human Warden session at the new frame.

## Hypothesis
We believe moving the frame radius from 2000 to 1500 uu raises the smallest player round in the 720p gate frame from 3.2 to 4.3 px and the swarm hull at 1080p from 17 to 23 px, while every attack still starts on screen with 0.8 m beyond a Tank's diameter to spare, because the down-screen reach falls from 20.1 to 15.0 m against a farthest attack start of 13.0 m. Measured at the implementation step (constants, framing test, contrast and smoke gates) and at the O-008 sessions, where no validator reports a hit from an unseen source; reverted to 2000 if any does, or if R-DIR-02's damage-taken band moves out at the new frame on human runs.

## Implemented on `main` (2026-11-10, branch `fix/d036-cam`)

# D-069 — The duos' door is earned by play: a run that holds six cards of two Keepers

- **Date:** 2026-11-12
- **Decider:** scheduler (delegated, D-009), on a finding and three options by the Mechanics module session while building D-064 (`s3/d064-fresh-pool`), 2026-11-12. The owner's word of 2026-11-11 applies: "ask for the reason, make your decision which helps the game the best"
- **Class:** RULE (`meta.md` R-MET-27, R-MET-28, R-MET-30 and T-MET-30, on `dev/s3`). It amends D-064's Impact and answers the Open row of `meta.md` on the duos' door
- **Status:** accepted; built with D-064 by the Mechanics session on `dev/s3`

## Context
- D-064 opens one whole archetype for each S1 Keeper on a fresh save: its Key was already there, and the two Amps and the Trait join it. A fresh save then holds six cards of each S1 Keeper.
- Six cards is Tier 2. The Discovery `TwoPaths6` (`Best.PathsAtTier2` at 2 or more, in one run) is therefore met by play on a fresh save. Its targets are `Mod.SpreadWave` and `Reaction.Duos`.
- Until D-064 a first run held three Keys a Keeper, so Tier 2 took a bought `Blessing4` on two Paths: the duos stood behind two purchases. `Validation/S3_doors.md` calls it "the one door that does not open today on a person's save", and `meta.md` holds it open: "is a bought depth the right door for a duo?"
- Measured by the Mechanics session on fresh bot runs (skill 1.0, steered arms, so a bound): `TwoPaths6` earned in 0 of 90 on the base and in 67 and 66 of 90 on the branch's two seed ranges.
- The gate found it: `Tools/EarnedCheck.ps1` (R-MET-30, step `earned`) asserts that the duos are not earned after run one. Every other step passed on the branch with `dev/s3` 00332f5 merged: 547 tests, the restart and meta hashes unmoved, `firstrun` six of six.
- D-064's dependency sweep named no Discovery. This record is that missing line.

## Options
- A — Accept it: the duos' door is earned by play, by a run that takes all six cards of two Keepers. The rules and the check say so.
- B — A fresh save opens the two Amps only; the Trait stays behind `Blessing4`. Five cards, Tier 2 out of reach, every door where it was. A first run then never completes an archetype.
- C — Leave the pool and raise the door's own threshold: a new number on a Discovery, set for pacing.

## Decision
A, in seven items.

1. `Reaction.Duos` and `Mod.SpreadWave` stay on `TwoPaths6`, and `TwoPaths6` is a door a person's first runs can open. No cell of `discoveries.csv` moves and no number moves; the pool widens (pillar 5 holds).
2. Why. The row's own words are two Paths at Tier 2. Earning it by building two Paths in a run is what the row says; earning it only after two purchases was a side effect of the old starter pool, and the spec already doubted it. B gives up the reason for D-064, a first run that can show one whole archetype. C invents a number to keep a side effect.
3. It fits the pace the other doors already have: a good first run opens the three S2 Keepers by `ReachL10`, `WeaponL8` and `Survive10`. Whether that pace is right is read once, for all doors together, at the S3 session.
4. A reading is owed before the branch lands: `-Check T-OFF-15` on the fullest pool a person can hold entering their second run, the first-run pool with the duos and Spread Wave, read with and without the three S2 Keepers a first run can also open. If the band or the Reserve cap fails there, the job stops and reports; nothing is built around it.
5. `Tools/EarnedCheck.ps1` is rewritten to the new rule, not loosened. Its purchase leg stays as it is. Its duo leg binds the rule from both sides on real launches: `TwoPaths6` is earned when and only when the run held Tier 2 on two Paths, read from the run's own counters, and the run after it is offered a duo card. Removing the old assertion without the other side is not accepted.
6. `meta.md` R-MET-27's row for `TwoPaths6`, the sentence of R-MET-28, R-MET-30 and T-MET-30 say so, and the Open row on the duos' door closes with this record.
7. D-064 stands as accepted, option b, and its revert clause stands as written. `Blessing4` no longer leads to the duos; it still opens a whole second archetype, and whether validators buy it is still that clause's measure.

## Hypothesis
We believe that a duos' door earned by play gives a person on a fresh save, within their first runs, a discovery that comes from building two Paths and not from a purchase, because a fresh save now holds six cards of each S1 Keeper and a steered bot at skill 1.0 earns the row in 133 of 180 fresh runs (a bound: a person takes cards less single-mindedly and dies sooner); measured before landing by item 4's reading, and at the S3 session by the run at which each validator's duos opened and by whether they can say what the first duo card they took did. Reverted, by moving `Reaction.Duos` to a deeper Discovery row, which is one cell of `discoveries.csv`, if two of three validators call the duo cards noise or cannot say what one did.

# D-040 — A dash volley never starts a live ring or beam again: one strike, the rule of Chain Trigger's free volley

- **Date:** 2026-11-10
- **Decider:** scheduler (delegated, D-009)
- **Class:** RULE
- **Status:** applied on `fix/dash-ring-shape`, cut from `dev/s2` at 846ceb1; test and harness readings only, no bot battery, no human reading

## Context
Two free volleys reach Pulse Wall and Sweep Beam, and they had two rules. D-038 clause 3 and R-RFG-08: a Quicksilver dash volley starts the live ring again at the hero, or the live beam again along the dash, as its own at `dmg_mult`. D-039: a kill's free volley (`Mod.ChainTrigger`) never does; the live ring strikes its band once more, the beam gives one tick, and what lives is left alone. D-039 measured the restart for a kill and found it erased the wall. A dash is far rarer than a kill, so the restart could be sound for a dash. Nobody had measured it; D-038's hypothesis left it to a person.

Measured here. `Dusktracer.Keepers.S2.Card.Quicksilver.Vector.DashMarch` is D-039's `.March` with a hero who dashes: six enemies a second walking in at 100 uu/s, kills resolved, pushes applied, the evolved weapon alone at level 8 with the Vector Key, two minutes, at 60 and 240 health. Each reading is the sum of four runs whose first dash falls 0.9 s apart, across one ring's life. Three dash rates: one every 33 s (the owner's one run, 1.81 a minute), every 9 s (the bot's: Vector:Forge at skill 1.0 presses 104.5 times in a run of about 970 s), and every 0.9 s, the least the Vector column allows (below). Four scenes: the hero stands; or each dash carries it 450 uu a new way (Roam), away from a crowd that comes from one side (Flee), or into it (Charge). Three shapes: no Key; the restart; one strike, D-039's shape at `dmg_mult` with the refund. The restart was read on 846ceb1 with only the tests added, the strike after the change. Breaches are enemy-steps within 150 uu of the hero. Every row is in `Validation/S2_dash_volley.md`.

Kills, no Key → restart → one strike, the hero standing:

What the readings say:
- **At the owner's rate and the bot's, standing, the restart does no harm.** Pulse Wall's wall holds (breaches 0 in every reading) and the restart is the larger gain, up to 2.8 % of kills against 0.9 %. Sweep Beam moves by about 1 % or less under either shape, which is the scene's own scatter.
- **Chained, the restart is D-039's fault again.** At a dash every 0.9 s Pulse Wall at 240 hp keeps 314 of 2436 kills and breaches go from 0 to 5858352; Sweep Beam loses 12 % and 24 % of its kills and breaches rise from 12504 to 1084212 and from 847920 to 3049851. One strike reads 2532, 2828 and 2620, with the wall whole.
- **0.9 s is the column's own floor, and the restart feeds it.** Vector Amp1 scales the dash cooldown by −0.20, to 2.4 s; the Key refunds up to `refund_max` of the base cooldown, 1.5 s, per dash. Under the restart the ring is the dash volley's for its whole life, so every kill it makes for 3.6 s refunds: 1.37 to 1.45 s per dash at both measured rates, against 0.26 to 0.50 s for one strike. A hero who dashes whenever the dash is ready, with the Key alone, dashes 243 times where the hero without it dashes 133 (read once, on a build that was not kept). Amp2's Tailwind (`tailwind_s` 1.0) pays for a dash every second.
- **A dash that moves the hero cuts both ways.** A dash volley fires at the dash start, so the restarted ring is centred on the spot the hero is leaving and holds it for a whole ring life, during which the weapon's own ring cannot open where the hero lands. At the bot's rate that reads worse dashing into the crowd (kills 1753 → 1504 at 60 hp, 186 → 34 at 240 hp; one strike 1781 and 193) and better dashing away (1763 → 1971 at 240 hp; one strike 1844). At the owner's rate no scene moves by more than 3 % of kills under either shape.
- **Most of what the restart credits to Quicksilver is the weapon's own ring.** Standing at the bot's rate, 42 % and 45 % of Pulse Wall's damage reads `Keeper.Quicksilver` while the total rises 4.8 % and 2.6 %; under one strike 4.8 % and 5.0 % of it does.

## Options
- **A. D-038 stands.** Sound at every rate a person or a bot has shown. It leaves a weapon the Vector column erases when its own cards are played as they ask, with nothing on screen to say why, and two rules for two free volleys on the same two weapons.
- **B. One strike for the dash volley too, on both weapons.** D-039's shape at `dmg_mult`, refund kept. No reading loses the wall or the sweep at any rate. The Key is smaller on these two weapons at ordinary rates.
- **C. One strike on Pulse Wall, the restart kept on Sweep Beam.** The beam's fault is the smaller one (a quarter of the kills at worst, not seven eighths). It keeps two rules, and the beam's restart also takes the weapon's aim: for `beam_s` it sweeps about the dash line and not the densest line.
- **D. Keep the restart and cap how often it may happen.** A new number, and the catalog is not to move.

## Decision
B. No catalog number is added or moved: both cases read `dmg_mult` and the weapon's own row.

1. **Pulse Wall.** While a ring lives, a dash volley strikes its band once more where it stands, for one tick of the row at `dmg_mult` (`dps` × `Damage` × `tick_s` × `dmg_mult`), as `Keeper.Quicksilver`, refund per kill. The ring's place, size, clock, own ticks and source are untouched. With no ring live the dash volley opens one, as before, and that ring is the dash volley's at `dmg_mult`. This replaces D-038 clause 3.
2. **Sweep Beam.** While a beam lives, a dash volley is one tick of the beam (`width_deg`, `reach_uu`, the weapon's tick damage × `dmg_mult`) along the dash line, as `Keeper.Quicksilver`, refund per kill; the live beam sweeps on. With no beam live it starts one along the dash, as before. This replaces the dash half of R-RFG-08's restart. Why the dash line and not the densest line: R-KEE-34 and the Key say "along the dash", and the plain Rail Lance's dash volley is one lance along the dash, which this tick is the like of. The densest line was measured too and kills as many; it would make a dash an aimed shot the card does not promise.
3. **One rule.** A free volley, a kill's or a dash's, never starts again what lives one per weapon. The two differ only in what each carries: its source, its multiplier and its aim.
4. **Everything else D-038 decided stands**: Blade Halo's blades, Cyclone's landing sweep, Singularity's merge.

Why B and not A: the two shapes differ little where the game has been played and by a wall where the Vector column points. The restart's gain at ordinary rates is partly the weapon's own ring under another name, and its refund is what shortens the dash toward the rate at which it breaks. A pick that hurts and never says so is what D-039 was written to remove; leaving the same fault behind a rarer trigger is not a different answer. One rule is also less code: `OpenForceField` loses its restart branch and is as it was before D-038.

What is given up: the larger gain standing or fleeing at ordinary rates (Pulse Wall kills +0.5 to +2.8 % standing under the restart, +0.1 to +0.9 % now; fleeing at the bot's rate +11.8 % against +4.6 %), and the size of the act. On these two weapons a dash is one strike, not a ring or a sweep; on Sweep Beam its worth in kills is inside the scene's scatter at ordinary rates, as the restart's was.

Card text: none changes. "Dashing fires every weapon along the dash at ×1.5" / "冲刺时所有武器沿冲刺路线齐射，伤害 ×1.5" stays true in the sense D-039 recorded for the modifier's line: the weapon acts once, at the multiplier, and on these two forms the act is one tick of what lives, not a whole ring or beam. No string moved and no loc file was rebuilt.

## Hypothesis
We believe making a dash volley strike once with Pulse Wall's live ring and Sweep Beam's live beam, with no restart, moves the kills of a standing hero who dashes every 0.9 s from 314 of 2436 (Pulse Wall, 240 hp) and 2010 of 2632 (Sweep Beam, 240 hp) to no fewer than 19 in 20 of the weapon's own, and costs no reading its wall, because `Vector.DashMarch` reads it so before and after; measured by T-KEE-34 now and by the first bot battery or human session that holds a Vector card with either weapon; reverted to the restart for Pulse Wall alone if that session reads the Key as a wasted pick on it and no build in it dashes faster than once in 1.5 s.

## Result
- Before the change, on 846ceb1 with only the tests added: 3 of the 12 `Vector.*` tests fail, on 12 lines. `Vector.PulseWall` (5: no strike, no tick at the multiplier, two strikes for one dash, the ring started again, the weapon's own ticks spent), `Vector.SweepBeam` (3: two ticks for one, the weapon's sweep cut from 2 hits to 0, and a dash across the line ends the sweep), `Vector.DashMarch` (4: Pulse Wall at 240 hp and Sweep Beam at both healths lose more than one kill in twenty at 0.9 s, and Pulse Wall's wall does not hold). After it all 12 pass, and the seven `Modifiers.ChainTrigger.*` tests still pass.
- Code, RULE class, sim layer, `DTWeaponSim.cpp` and `.h` only: `FireVolley`'s two branches that D-039 gave a kill's free volley now take any free volley, each at the multiplier the volley carries; `OpenForceField` loses `bRestart` and its restart branch. It replaces the dash volley's restart of the ring and of the beam; nothing is stacked beside them.

# D-059 — The Forge and Quicksilver trails keep their colours, and a colour-vision debt is read on the drawn frame

- **Date:** 2026-11-11
- **Decider:** scheduler, on the owner's word. Asked O-015 in the scheduler's session on 2026-11-11, the owner answered: "ask for the reason, make your decision which helps the game the best"
- **Class:** RULE (presentation; a method). It closes O-015
- **Status:** applied: no token moves. The evidence is still outside the repo and is owed as a note (Impact)

## Context
`settings.md` R-SET-11 (on `dev/s2` and later) asks that a player colour and an enemy colour stay apart under simulated colour blindness: 15 against the enemy muzzle flash (`C.SETTINGS.CVD_MUZZLE_DELTA_E_MIN`), 20 otherwise (`C.SETTINGS.CVD_DELTA_E_MIN`), as CIE76 after the Machado 2009 simulation. Worked out from the palette values, the Forge trail (`Pal.Keeper.Forge` #FF9F3A) fails twice under deutan vision: 8.5 against the muzzle and 16.5 against the telegraph. The spec names both as debts and its Open section calls the fix "an owner call". O-015 put two options: A, the source game's lighter pair for the Forge and Quicksilver trails (#FFC17A, #B3FB60); B, keep the palette the owner confirmed (D-008) and rely on the Danger outlines setting.

The Visual session shot both on `dev/s4` at 1920x1080 in a staged fight (four weapons at level 8, 48 Turrets, 12 Rushers, held at 5.6 s), plain and through the simulation, and measured them with the pipeline of `Dusktracer.Settings.ColourVision`. The scheduler looked at the crops. What they show:

| Forge trail, deutan | Against the muzzle (asked 15) | Against the telegraph (asked 20) |
|---|---|---|
| From the palette values, today | 8.5 | 16.5 |
| From the palette values, option A | 20.3 | 27.2 |
| Measured on the drawn frame, today | 17.0 | 31.8 |
| Measured on the drawn frame, option A | 17.6 | 32.5 |

- A trail is thin, see-through and drawn over the floor, so it never shows its palette colour. The arithmetic says A gains about 11; on the screen it gains under 1, and today's trail already clears both limits there.
- A is paid for elsewhere. Forge against the Radiance trail falls from 24.8 to 7.5 by the palette under deutan (38.6 to 17.5 for normal vision), and from 17.7 to 15.0 on the drawn frame (21.1 to 16.8). In the sheet the two trails cannot be told apart under the simulation.
- Danger is told by shape in every frame, simulated or not: an enemy round is a ring with a dark middle, a telegraph is a band with a black edge, a player round is a white head with a hairline trail.
- The muzzle flash the first debt is measured against is about 120 to 150 pixels of two million.
- One figure runs the other way: today's Quicksilver trail reads 13.8 against the muzzle on the drawn frame, under the 15 asked, and A lifts it to 16.0. It rests on the same few muzzle pixels.

## Options
- A — Change the two trails to the lighter pair. It clears the arithmetic, changes almost nothing a deutan player sees, and brings Forge and Radiance together.
- B — Keep the palette and rely on the outlines setting. Nothing changes; the two debts stay written as debts that the screen does not show.
- C — Keep the palette, and move the measure to where the player looks: the drawn frame.

## Decision
C, in five items.

1. `Pal.Keeper.Forge` and `Pal.Keeper.Quicksilver` keep their values. D-008 stands.
2. Danger is told by shape before colour, as it is today, and the Danger outlines setting stays the choice of a player who wants more.
3. A colour-vision debt between a player mark and an enemy mark is judged on a drawn frame. The palette arithmetic of T-SET-11 stays in the test as the early warning it is, with its two debts named. A presentation package that puts a player colour near an enemy colour reads the pair on a staged frame under the three simulations, by the same pipeline, and reports the figure beside the palette's. The Burn layer of the second visual wave is the first to owe it (D-057 ruling 9).
4. The Open line of `settings.md` that calls this an owner call is closed with this record, at the Mechanics session's next change on `dev/s2`.
5. The Quicksilver figure is not acted on: it is one frame and a handful of pixels. It is read again on the first frame where a muzzle flash is large enough to measure.

## Hypothesis
We believe keeping the palette costs a deutan player nothing they can see and keeps two Keepers' trails apart, because on the drawn frame the lighter pair moves the Forge trail by under one unit against the muzzle and the telegraph and by about three toward the Radiance trail; measured by those drawn-frame figures, and, when a player with deutan vision is at hand, by whether they tell a Forge trail from an enemy round and a telegraph in play. Reverted, to option A or to a new pair, if such a player cannot, or if a drawn-frame reading on a later frame (a smaller screen, the second weather, a thicker trail) falls under the limits of R-SET-11.

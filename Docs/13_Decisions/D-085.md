# D-085 — The field the Reforging band is read on is as busy as play; a card is read on two sides

- **Date:** 2026-11-13
- **Decider:** scheduler (delegated, D-009), on the Mechanics module session's Draft 20 (`Validation/reforge_field_health_plan.md`, `plan/reforge-field-health` 1da47d1) and the five stages of the equipment's first reading
- **Class:** METHOD. It amends D-082 items 1, 2 and 10 and says how the band of `reforging.md` R-RFG-18 is judged. No rule of play and no number of the game
- **Status:** accepted; `Tools/probe.py` and its tests on `dev/s2`, in the equipment's branch or behind it; the stage at the fixed health is read at once

## Context
- D-082 reads the band on the probe's fixed field against no pick and says: raise the field's health until no scene saturates. The equipment's first reading (three hold batteries' level 16 snapshots, 45 builds, on `s2/reforge-band-equipment` 34c6ae5 before its gate) was read at five healths, 10, 20, 40, 80 and 160 times the health play gives a body at that minute. No card is judged by it.
- The lift by damage, in that order: Storm's card 1.42, 1.41, 1.36, 1.33, 1.32; Tide's 1.12, 1.17, 1.27, 1.30, 1.33; Blade Halo 1.27, 1.36, 1.46, 1.40, 1.40; Pulse Wall 1.91, 2.03, 2.83, 3.30, 3.28; Murmuration 1.98, 2.38, 2.79, 4.23, 5.05. The build without a pick kills 98, 27, 1, 0 and 0 bodies in the window at the median; in play the same builds kill 43 in ten seconds at the hand's minute.
- So for a card that strikes many bodies the lift rises with the field's health and does not settle while anything still dies; from 80 times on nothing dies and the figure is a card's output on bodies that never die, its best case; at the low end the field empties and every strong card is pressed to one figure. "Raise it until no scene saturates" has no stopping point that is not a choice, and the choice moves the two crowd cards by a factor of two and a half.
- Two facts of the field, found on the way: it has no hard ceiling (a dead body returns the same step on a ring 1 600 uu out and walks in for 5.6 s; one scene killed 669), so the flag of 160 kills was an old reading's lower edge; and the window's damage is applied damage (`telemetry.md` R-TEL-11), so it never held overkill.
- By the boss's own measure the same cards read near the Path's card in play (the wall 1.07, Murmuration 0.99), and before the boss play is supply-bound (D-081).

## Options
- **A.** D-082 as written: raise the health until no scene saturates.
- **B.** A health for each snapshot, each build on its own field.
- **C.** One health for a reading, the one at which the build without a pick kills as fast as it does in play.

## Decision
C, as drafted, with rulings 9 to 11.

1. **The health is fixed by the rate play kills at.** The play rate is the median, over the runs whose snapshots are read, of the kills in the minute that holds the Reforging hand, scaled to the probe's window. The field's health is the one at which the no-pick scenes of those snapshots kill that many at the median, found on the series 5, 10, 20 and 40 by interpolation between the two stages that bracket it, on the logarithm of both, rounded to a whole multiple; that stage is then read whole.
2. **Once for a reading, not for each snapshot,** so every card of a reading stands on one field. A reading on other Paths finds its own health the same way and says it.
3. **Why this point.** Lower, the field is empty; higher, nothing dies, a fight the game does not have. Here the build without a pick is as busy as it is in play. The cost is said in every reading's note: the bodies are tougher than play's (about fifteen times by these stages), because play's stream cannot occupy a level 16 build at all.
4. **Room above.** If the play rate is over half the standing field (60 of 120), the health is raised until it is not, and the reading says so.
5. **Applied damage is what is judged.** The lift by kills is printed beside it wherever the no-pick median is twenty kills or more.
6. **"At least".** A scene that kills more than the 120 that stand has drawn on the ring; its figure is printed as "at least". The flag of 160 leaves the check.
7. **The far end is printed and never judged:** one stage where the build without a pick kills nothing, named "bodies that do not die": what a card is worth where the game is harder than its stream.
8. **D-082's check, item 10, fourth clause** becomes: the reading names its play rate, the stages that bracket it, the health it fixed, and how many scenes read "at least".
9. **A card is read on two sides,** and this is how the band is judged from now on: against a crowd, on this field; against one body, by the damage dealt to the boss a minute against the arm that declines the hand (D-081 item 1). In band means at least `REFORGE.POWER_MIN` on one side and at most `REFORGE.POWER_MAX` on both. A card for one body is not failed by a crowd's measure; a card for crowds is not passed by the field alone if it multiplies a build there. The play floors of D-082 item 4 stay on both sides for every card.
10. **A split is said, not settled by the tool.** Where the lift by damage and the lift by kills fall on different sides of a bound, the reading marks the card split, and the record on what an evolution must give decides it.
11. **No card is judged before the fixed stage is read,** and none is changed by a reading alone (D-082 item 11). The figures by arithmetic in the draft (a health of about 16 times; Storm's card about 1.41, Tide's 1.15, the halo 1.33, the wall 1.99, Murmuration 2.23) are not readings and go into no verdict.

## Hypothesis
None: a method. It is kept if one reading can say of each card which side of each bound it stands on, on a field whose busyness is play's; reopened if the fixed health proves unstable from one battery of the same pair to the next (the play rate or the bracket moving the health by more than a stage of the series), or if the two sides cannot both be read for a card.

## Impact (dependency sweep)
- `Tools/probe.py`: the calibration, the "at least" mark, the far stage, the reader's lines; its tests. Tools only. `S2Exit.ps1`'s band step calls the stage with the calibration.
- D-082: items 1, 2 and 10 as amended here; its item 11 and its floors stand. `reforging.md` R-RFG-18's sentence on what the band is measured on names the two sides.
- The S2 exit battery: the band's step reads sixteen cards on the fixed field and the boss's side against the arm that declines; thirteen cards and every boss side against no pick are unread today.
- What the stages suggest and no more: the two Path cards and the halo stand near the lower bound against a crowd; the wall and Murmuration stand over the upper bound against a crowd at every health read and level with the Path's card against one body. Play today hides that because play is supply-bound; a harder mode would not.
- Not shown: the stage at the fixed health; whether a field of tougher bodies stands for a harder game well enough to judge a card by. People will say what the cards are worth.

# D-080 — The camera is raised: the hero stands above the centre, the reach is equal up and down, and the frame tightens to about 45 m

- **Date:** 2026-11-12
- **Decider:** scheduler, on the owner's word at the sitting of 2026-11-12 ("你来定", you decide, asked about the camera) and D-077 item 7; from the Visual session's camera study (eight framings, frames and tables, 2026-11-12) and the Mechanics session's sums and its table of what each framing makes stale
- **Class:** RULE (`06_UX` R-UX-01; the values under it are a PRESENT change). It amends D-036: its fairness line and its fixed frame stand; its centred aim and its frame radius are replaced
- **Status:** applied on `main` (2026-11-13); the alone perf pair and the fifth session package follow; then carried to every branch

## Context
- The owner called the game crude and left the camera to the scheduler. The owner's art direction draws its fight about 27 m across with the hero about 60 px tall. The game frames 53 m (D-036) and its figure measures 49 px.
- D-036 chose today's frame as the tightest CENTRED fixed frame in which every enemy attack starts on screen: the down-screen reach from the hero, the shortest in any direction, must hold 1 300 uu, and 1 416 with a Tank's diameter (R-UX-01). The study and the sums confirm it: a centred frame can tighten to a radius of 1 300 at most, and then with no margin.
- The art direction's 27 m does not fit this fight: half of every Turret's range and of the Warden's hazard ring, most drop-in markers and three quarters of the Ring Emitter's own circle would be off screen. To use it the fight would be rescaled whole, which is a new first stage with every exit and every human session again. It is not taken.
- Nothing in the sim reads the frame; the bot is blind to it; manual aim deprojects the cursor onto the sim plane, so where the hero stands on the screen does not move the aim. No bot reading, hash or gate value is stale at any frame. Every human reading is taken at a frame, and one human reading exists (the owner's run); session A is not held yet.
- The lens at pitch 60: the floor reach is 1.36 frame radii up the screen and 1.00 down. Today's frame therefore holds 1 992 uu up and 1 518 down on the plane bodies are drawn on: about 470 uu of reach up the screen that the fairness line does not ask for.
- The study's raised frames aim the camera at a point shifted toward the camera, so that the reach up equals the reach down. With an equal reach of 1 416 uu (frame radius 1 213, shift 186) the figure measures 62 px, the frame is 43.6 m across and is filled by the fight; Turret fire, the hazard ring and the drop-in markers all start on screen; the outer spawn circle is 63 parts in a hundred in view where today's is 85. With an equal reach of 1 300 (radius 1 115) the figure is 68 px and the margin of a Tank's width is gone: a source at the farthest start sits on the edge for a quarter of bearings and a sixth of the Ring Emitter's circle ends off screen.
- The HUD takes reach. A pixel at the top of the screen hides 3.55 uu and one at the bottom 1.93. On the hero's column only the boss bar covers anything: 29 rows at the top, 103 uu, and only in the boss fight, where the hazard ring matters most.
- Drawn size against sim size, on crops: the hero drawn at 1.25 of today's size on its unchanged hit circle is plainly found and one enemy round touches its helm's edge; at 1.5 rounds sit over helm and shoulder with no hit, which lies about what can be hit. A Swarm pack drawn at 1.25 merges into one lump; at 1.0 the hulls stand apart.
- Not verified by anyone: play. Every figure is a held frame or a sum.

## Options
- **Keep D-036's frame** and gain size by drawn size alone. The fight keeps 470 uu of floor up the screen that nothing needs, and enemies cannot be drawn larger without merging.
- **The raised frame at the fairness line as written** (equal reach 1 416), the reach counted to the HUD's inner edge.
- **The raised frame with no body margin** (equal reach 1 300): the art direction's hero size at once, paid for with the margin D-036 put there on purpose.
- **A centred frame at a radius of 1 300**: a seventh larger, the margin gone, the surplus up the screen kept.
- **The art direction's 27 m**: see Context.

## Decision
The raised frame at the fairness line as written, counted to the HUD.

1. **The rule.** R-UX-01 becomes: the reach from the hero on the plane bodies are drawn on is equal up the screen and down the screen, and each is at least the farthest attack start plus a Tank's diameter (1 416 uu today), counted down to the bottom edge and up to the lower edge of whatever the HUD draws across the hero's column (the boss bar). The frame is the tightest that meets it at pitch 60 and field of view 50. The numbers follow from the rule: a frame radius of about 1 258 uu and an aim shift of about 139 uu toward the camera, which is about 45 m across at 42 px a metre, the figure about 59 px tall where it is 49 today, and the hero about 60 px above the centre of a 1080-row screen. The Mechanics session gives the exact pair; the test holds the rule, not the two numbers.
2. **Fixed, as before.** R-UX-02 stands whole: no zoom, no player setting, and the shift is a constant like the radius. The hero's place on the screen never moves.
3. **Why not the tighter one.** The margin of a Tank's width is what makes a source a body on screen and not a sliver at the edge; D-036 paid for it knowingly and nothing has been read that buys it back. The remaining distance to the art direction's figure is closed by drawn size, which costs no fairness.
4. **Drawn size.** A hull is drawn at 1.0 on its hit circle: enemies are many, and a pack drawn larger merges. The hero's figure may be drawn up to 1.25 of the size its hit circle would give and never more; the value is set with the new figure's frames (D-077 ruling 22) and until then today's figure stays at 1.0. A drawn size is a draw-side parameter and no sim number.
5. **It lands on `main`, before session A.** The frame is the first stage's rule and its constant lives there. Session A is the first time people are measured, and every human reading is taken at a frame: D-036 was landed before the session for that reason, and a frame ruled today and landed after the session would make the session's readings on damage taken, on dashes and on seeing an attack stale before they were read. It is not a defect of the build, and D-050's line is crossed here on purpose and for this reason only. The session's package is built once, after this and D-078 have both landed, unless the owner names a date sooner.
6. **The job**, `s1/camera-raised` from `main`, by a developer of the Visual session who wrote the study's switches: the two constants (the radius, and a new `ARENA.CAMERA_AIM_SHIFT_UU`) with their number-log rows; `ADTCameraPawn`; whatever in the HUD's layout places a mark on a body; `06_UX.md` R-UX-01 and T-UX-01 as item 1 words them; T-UX-02 unchanged in meaning; the goldens of `main` shot again once and each difference listed; the perf scenes read as a pair in turn, before and after; the whole gate. No sim file is touched: the gate's five arms must not move, and the job stops if one does.
7. **The carry.** `dev/s4` takes it first, so that the look is built at the frame it will be seen at; every golden on the later branches is shot again in the carry. The look's sheets hold at this frame from now on; "the raised 39 m frame" in rulings 18 to 22 of D-077 is replaced by this one.
8. **The Warden and its bar.** In the study's boss frame the Warden's hull runs up under the boss bar and the bar lies over its optic: a tall thing up the screen needs more room than a sum on the floor gives. That is designed in the Warden's own package: its height, and the bar's place, which may move to the bottom centre where a row hides half as much. Until then the bar stays and the rule of item 1 counts it.
9. **The owner's look.** The owner is asked to play the frame once on `main`'s build when it has landed; nothing waits on it.

## Hypothesis
We believe that the raised frame makes the figure and every hull about a fifth larger at no cost in fairness, because it spends only the reach up the screen that the fairness line never asked for; measured by the camera's own test (the equal reach, at least 1 416 uu to the HUD's inner edge) and by the frames of the three staged scenes before and after; judged by the owner's look and by session A's people: reverted to D-036's two values if a person reports a hit from off screen, or cannot follow the hero, in a way the old frame did not show.

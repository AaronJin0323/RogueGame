# D-013 — S1 spec set: integration calls

- **Date:** 2026-10-30
- **Decider:** scheduler (delegated, D-009)
- **Class:** SYSTEM
- **Status:** applied

## Context
Five S1 specs were written in parallel (keepers, weapons/modifiers, offers/progression, combat_core/director, enemies/boss) and reconciled in one integration pass. A few seams needed a call rather than a fix.

## Decision
1. **Patron at run start.** The primary Keeper is picked in an unweighted run-start Patron hand of all S1 Keepers (offers R-OFF-08, keepers R-KEE-05); it is not counted in `C.DECISIONS.PER_RUN`. Reason: validators must name their archetype; an explicit pick makes the identity visible from the first second and matches the future hub.
2. **Second-Keeper legality** is the `IncompatFlags` column of `09_Catalog/expression.csv` (keepers R-KEE-16); `flag_compat.csv` is weapon capability × flag only.
3. **L16 Reforging in S1** has no transformation source (evolutions and transformations are S2): the hand is the three highest-weight legal blessings of the two chosen Keepers (offers R-OFF-09). It stays a real three-way choice.
4. **Starter pools in S1:** weapons Pulse Rifle, Ring Emitter, Orbital Drones, Arc Blade (CORE cap 4); 8 modifiers; 9 archetype keys. Gravity Mine and the Fork and Ricochet modifiers are reachable only through `DT.Unlock` until meta (S3). Tide's identity rides on its flags, not the mine.
5. **One owner per number:** statuses → `enemies.md` (`C.STATUS.*`), role shares → `director.md`, gem cap → `progression.md`; world-view fields and `FDTHit` are defined once in `07_Technical §4`.
6. **Arrival attacks complete after their unit dies** (kill horizon, director R-DIR-07, enemies R-ENM-13). Checked in the first S1 session.

## Hypothesis
We believe the explicit Patron pick plus flag-driven archetypes lets ≥ 2 of 3 S1 validators name their archetype; measured at the S1 sessions (O-008).

## Impact
`offers.md`, `keepers.md`, `weapons.md`, `modifiers.md`, `enemies.md`, `director.md`, `progression.md`, `07_Technical.md`, `telemetry.md`; catalogs `weapons.csv`, `modifiers.csv`, `enemies.csv`.

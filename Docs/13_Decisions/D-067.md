# D-067 — Pulse Wall pushes out what its ring crosses

- **Date:** 2026-11-12
- **Decider:** scheduler (delegated, D-009), on a draft by the Mechanics module session (D-055 item 4: draft 9, `Validation/pulse_wall_band_plan.md`), accepted on 2026-11-12 with the rulings at the end
- **Class:** RULE (`reforging.md` R-RFG-12, on `dev/s2`). No number moves. It follows D-061, whose sweep showed the number is not the cause
- **Status:** accepted; built by the Mechanics session on `dev/s2`

## Context
- The card: "Rings hold as a wall, pushing enemies out." The source: "Rings expand into a slow persistent wall ring (r 600, 3 s) that pushes enemies out", and in its design log "each volley is one zone that grows to r 600 over 0.6 s, holds 3 s and knocks radially outward". The source's wall is a zone: what the growing ring covers is knocked out.
- The rule as ported, R-RFG-12: the ring strikes and pushes only "each enemy in its outer `band_uu`"; "what stands inside it is untouched". The band is the outer 100 uu of 600. The band, the push speed and the damage were first guesses of the port (`reforging.csv`: "push band dps first guess").
- The ring grows at about 1 000 uu a second and the push is 200 uu a second, so an enemy the front passes is in the band for a tenth of a second and moves about 20 uu. A Swarm enemy walks at 280 uu a second: it crosses the held band against the push.
- What the sweep read (D-061): at `dps` 2, 4, 6 and 8 the build deals 0.78, 0.80, 0.78 and 0.76 of its hold, while the ring's own damage rises from 0.65 to 1.19 of the plain weapon's. The number is not the cause.
- Where the damage goes, after the pick, hold against the evolved arm at `dps` 2 and 8:
  - the nearest enemy stands at 477 uu in the hold and at 194 and 199 uu in the arm;
  - live enemies 14 against 37 and 28;
  - damage taken per minute 4.0 against 24.7 and 17.1, most of it from Rushers and Tanks;
  - Keeper.Storm's damage 8 519 against 7 496 and 6 685 a minute: the rings were its carrier;
  - the arm wins 1 and 3 of 15 where the hold wins 15 (printed, judged nowhere).
- So the wall does not hold the crowd at its band. The crowd stands inside it, where nothing of the evolved weapon reaches, and the weapon that cleared the hero's surroundings at every range is gone. A player who reads the card expects the opposite.
- `reforging.md` R-RFG-18: a transformation changes how a build kills; its output after the pick stays in the source's band, `REFORGE.POWER_MIN` 1.3 to `REFORGE.POWER_MAX` 1.8. The bot's reading of that band is 1.00 "which may be a field cap".

## Options
- A — The whole disc hurts: the band becomes the full radius, so everything inside takes the ring's damage. No push inside. It turns the wall into a damage field the card does not describe, and the crowd still stands on the hero.
- B — The growing ring carries out what it crosses. While the ring grows, each enemy its front reaches is struck once, for one tick of the row, and is moved outward with the front through displacements, as far as the band's inner edge. While it holds, the band strikes and pushes as today. This is the source's zone and the card's sentence. It adds no number.
- C — The weapon keeps its rings and the wall is added to them. The build cannot lose output by the pick, and Keeper.Storm keeps its carrier; the wall stays as leaky as it is, and "rings hold as a wall" is no longer what happens.
- D — Leave the rule and hold for people (D-020's first answer). The card stays a pick that costs the run.

## Decision
B.

- R-RFG-12 gains: while the ring grows, an enemy its front reaches is struck once for one tick of the row and is carried outward with the front through displacements, no further than the band's inner edge; an enemy that ignores displacement (the boss) is struck and stays. The sentence "what stands inside it is untouched" becomes: what stands inside a held ring is untouched until the next ring opens.
- Nothing else in the rule moves: one ring per weapon, the cooldown at least the ring's life, the band's strike and push while it holds, the free volley's one more strike (D-039, D-040), the two beaded arcs.
- No number moves: the strike on the way out is the row's own tick (`dps` times one round's damage times `tick_s`), and the front's speed is `radius_uu` over `grow_s`. `dps` stays 2.
- Why B and not C: C keeps the build's output by leaving the old weapon in place, which is not a transformation. Why not A: it answers the output and not the crowd on the hero, and it is not what the card says.
- What B may do to output, said now so the reading is not a surprise: with the crowd held in the band, every enemy there takes the row's damage at once. In a static crowd of 24 the band already deals about twice the plain weapon's damage. If the reading lands above 1.8, the answer is one TUNE pass on `dps` downward by `10_Tuning.md §5`, as its own step after this rule has its reading; D-061's sweep already shows how the ring's damage follows the cell.
- What B may do to danger: a ring follows a ring, so walkers are swept out every cycle and Tanks, which the push outruns, do not reach the hero while the weapon is held. Rushers still charge through, Turret fire still arrives, the boss does not move, and a Swarm enemy still crosses the held band. Whether that is too safe is a person's reading at the first S2 session; the bot at skill 1.0 already takes almost nothing with the plain weapon (4.0 a minute), so it cannot show it.

## Hypothesis
We believe carrying out what the ring crosses moves the nearest enemy after the pick from about 195 uu to at least the band's inner edge at the median, and the build's damage per minute from 0.78 of its hold to at least 1.0, because the crowd then stands where the wall strikes and no longer on the hero; measured by `Tools/BotRuns.ps1 -Carry` on the Ring Emitter pair, 15 seeds, bot v2 at skill 1.0, read as `Validation/S2_d061_pulse_wall.md` read it (damage by source, nearest enemy, live enemies, damage taken; reach and wins printed and judged nowhere); reverted, the clause removed, if the nearest enemy still stands inside the band's inner edge at the median or the build stays under its hold.

## Impact (dependency sweep)
- Rules replaced: R-RFG-12 gains the clause and loses "what stands inside it is untouched" as an absolute; its test line gains the cases (an enemy inside an opening ring ends at the band and is struck once; an enemy that ignores displacement is struck and stays; a held ring leaves its inside alone).
- Constants: none. Catalog: none.
- Files / tests: `02_Systems/reforging.md`, `Combat/DTWeaponSim.cpp` (the force field's growth), the Reforging tests. The card's text stays true and does not change. The drawing does not change.
- Readings that move: runs that evolve the Ring Emitter, and no other. No run of the S2 exit battery does.
- Presentation: none asked. If the sweep outward should be seen (enemies sliding with the front), that is a Visual question for a later wave.

## Swap-out point
The first S2 session with people: whether a Pulse Wall build feels like a wall, and whether it is too safe.

## Accepted with these rulings (scheduler, 2026-11-12)
1. Accepted as drafted: option B. The card says the rings push enemies out, the source's wall is a zone that knocks outward, and the port's ring passes an enemy in a tenth of a second: the rule is what is wrong, and B makes the card true without a new number.
2. The reading is the hypothesis' own: the nearest enemy at the median and the build against its hold, on the same carry pair. Reach and wins are printed and judged nowhere (D-050 item 5).
3. If output lands above `REFORGE.POWER_MAX`, the TUNE pass on `dps` downward is a step of its own after this rule has its reading, as the draft says; it is not folded into this job.
4. Whether a wall that works is too safe is a question for the first S2 session with people, and the S2 kit asks it.
5. A displacement applied to a crowd every ring is new load on the sim: the job reads the S2 perf scene's game thread before and after as a bound and says so in its note.

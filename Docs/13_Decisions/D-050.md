# D-050 — Until people have played: the S1 build first, S4 wave 1 continues, nothing further starts early

- **Date:** 2026-11-11
- **Decider:** scheduler (delegated, D-009; the owner on 2026-11-11: "make the best decision for the game and fix docs, then tell development agents")
- **Class:** GO-NOGO
- **Status:** applied (this commit: `OPEN.md`, `STATE.md`, `12_Production §3`). It answers O-010

## Context
`Validation/plans_audit.md` 3.1, 3.4 to 3.6, 4.1 and 4.6.

- Four stages stand on no outside player and one owner run. Each early start was recorded (D-022, D-043) and none waives an exit, but nothing has brought an exit nearer since 2026-10-30.
- D-043's first wave is bounded with care: no sim number or rule moves, nothing merges back, art is judged at both frames, the contrast margin is watched.
- The build validators will play is `main`, and `main` is behind: the gate fixes are on `dev/s3` only, the zh audit is on `dev/s2` and up, two S2 sim defects were fixed on `dev/s3` only (406268e).
- About 35 of 43 tuning hypotheses were judged on a bot survival clause; three of 37 balance tunes met theirs. On the owner's own path the bot loses about 7 % of a bar; the owner lost all of it.
- One spec took three RULE records in an afternoon (D-038 to D-040).
- Every reading comes from one PC whose CPU faulted under heavy parallel load, and the perf lock is per worktree.

## Options
- A — Stop S4 until the exits. The plain reading of `Harness/H03`; agents idle while the only blocker is people.
- B — Let everything run as it is.
- C — Let wave 1 run, put the build people will play ahead of it, and stop the stack growing.

## Decision
C, in eight items.

1. **Wave 1 continues as D-043 scoped it.** No second wave, no S5 or later stage, and no new content on any branch starts before session A of D-048 has been held and read.
2. **Order of work.** First what validators will play: the consolidation list for `main` (`STATE.md` Queue). Then the carry of `main` to `dev/s2`, `dev/s3` and `dev/s4`. Then S4 wave 1. A consolidation job takes a merge and gate slot ahead of an S4 package.
3. **Fixes land on the lowest branch that has the defect** and are carried forward by merge in the order `main`, `dev/s2`, `dev/s3`, `dev/s4`. A fix found on a later branch for an earlier stage's system is made on the earlier branch.
4. **Three things run alone.** A package built for people, an exit battery and a perf reading run with no other engine or compiler process alive on the PC.
5. **No number is tuned on a bot survival clause** (boss reach, wins, median end) until the S1 gate decision names the skill row that brackets people. BUG fixes and D-045 to D-047 are not tunes and carry their own measures.
6. **One rule change per spec at a time.** A second RULE record on a spec waits until the first has a reading. This is `Harness/H04 §6` in the only form it can take before a validation cycle exists.
7. **Landing.** `dev/s2` lands on `main` when S1 exits and `dev/s3` when S2 exits. Until then each branch stays its stage's build, and validators play the package of their stage's branch.
8. **Floor and light work on `dev/s4`** reads contrast on the darkest and the brightest floor patch under each element, not on two percentiles of a flat floor. Today's seven elements all clear `C.PRE.CONTRAST_MIN` only where ground luminance is at most 0.037 or between 0.106 and 0.16 (`Validation/plans_audit.md` 5.10).

## Hypothesis
We believe putting the S1 build first moves the time from a booked session to a readable one from unknown to the length of the consolidation list, because every item on that list is something a validator would otherwise meet; measured by whether session A is read on a package built after the list; reverted, item by item, by the owner.

# D-019 — Frost and Stillness casts drop Tide's line

- **Date:** 2026-10-31
- **Decider:** scheduler (delegated, D-009)
- **Class:** RULE
- **Status:** applied, with the logged cast clause not met (see Result)

## Context
1. Tide's base pulls every enemy into a line ahead of the hero, then Freezes it. Every Tide variant ran that base, so a Frost or Stillness cast also moved the crowd out of the build's reach (`S1_keeper_power.md` §1.1 A2, §6).
2. The source text, read before building: `intercession_variants_spec.md` §2 gives Whirlpool "pulled into one point", Frost "Everything on screen Freezes 4 s", Stillness "Plants a tide pool". `ST_Ultimates` (Handoff) and `ST_Keepers` (`Tide.Frost.Ult.Desc`, `Tide.Stillness.Ult.Desc`) say the same. Neither names a pull. §1 rule 3 says a variant "changes the shape". The §2.1 layers that name "the line" do not conflict: they act on the variant's output shape and the variant wins a conflict.

## Decision
R-KEE-14: a Tide variant that names no `Flag.Pull` drops the line. Frost and Stillness Freeze the crowd where it stands; Whirlpool and the base still move it. The Freeze is unchanged on both, Stillness's screen Freeze included. Replaces the line on those two variants; no number moves.

## Hypothesis
Recorded in `number_change_log.csv` before measuring: the 13 Frost and Stillness combinations' mean rises from −8 506 to ≥ −1 500, and no Tide arm loses more than 2 of 15 at the boss (else rejected and reverted).

## Result
`Docs/Validation/S1_tide_rule.md`.

- **Boss clause met:** no Tide arm fell more than 1; the six Tide arms read 43/29 → 45/30.
- **Cast clause not met:** −2 203 against the −1 500 line. The line was set from the trial's −392, and that trial was a different rule: it had also taken the screen Freeze off Stillness. Built and measured again, that form reproduces all 13 trial rows, −392. So the line was aimed at the wrong number, and that is said here rather than moved after reading.
- **Why it stands despite the miss:** the scheduler's own rejection test for this rule is the boss clause, and it passed. The −1 500 clause was added on top by the implementer. The scheduler upholds it: the rule follows the source text, its reason was never the cast mean, and the miss comes from a target taken off the wrong trial.
- **Second form not adopted:** dropping Stillness's screen Freeze goes past this decision, which names the pull only, and it costs more at the boss (Tide arms 40/30; Tide-Frost-Storm 9 of 15). It remains a candidate: no text names that Freeze either.

## Impact
`keepers.md` R-KEE-14 table, T-KEE-21 (`Param.TideLineNeedsAPull`); `DTKeeperSim.cpp` `TidePull`.

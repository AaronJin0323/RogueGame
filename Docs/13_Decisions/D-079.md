- A blade's hit is worth what a bolt's is. The blades do not fail on what they touch; they touch little. A Swarm in the ring is dead within a second, and the rest of the build kills over nine in ten before the blades meet them, so few live bodies reach the ring. Standing closer did not help: the arm's second build landed fewer hits and fewer kills.
- So D-075's bar of 180 hits a minute measured supply and not use, and no bot standing anywhere could have met it in this build. The arm's two misses stand by its clause; the clause was wrong in kind. That is recorded here and changes nothing that was decided.
- The cause behind D-074's item 7: in a build that kills at range, a contact weapon's output is capped by what leaks through. A contact evolution can add to a ranged build, which is this record's form, or carry a build that is itself contact; it cannot replace a ranged weapon's emission. The source's halo traded 84 kills a minute at range for 22 at the ring, and what it no longer killed at range is the damage taken.
- This goes into the record of item 9 on what an evolution must give: a contact evolution is judged as an addition, and a build that is itself contact does not exist in this game yet.

# D-051 — The owner sees what was decided for them; decisions live on `main`; the number log's tags are named

- **Date:** 2026-11-11
- **Decider:** scheduler (delegated, D-009; the owner on 2026-11-11: "make the best decision for the game and fix docs, then tell development agents")
- **Class:** RULE (method). It amends D-009
- **Status:** applied (this commit: `OPEN.md`, `INDEX.md`, `10_Tuning.md §6`, `STATE.md`); item 2's back-fill by the next carry

## Context
`Validation/plans_audit.md` 3.3, 3.5 and 3.6.

- D-009 "replaces the daily owner window". `Harness/H08 §2` says a full-auto mandate does not remove it. Of the 35 decisions since D-009 the owner made one, and calls the harness keeps with the owner arrived as clauses in `STATE.md`.
- No branch holds every decision file: 28 on `main`, more on each later branch. `STATE.md` on `main` cites records `main` does not have, and the "next free number" line was wrong on two branches.
- The number log's tags mean the reverse of `Harness/H07 §6`: `[M]` marks bot and probe readings, `[H]` marks first values with no source, `[G]` was never defined. No row on any branch carries evidence from people, and the log cannot show that.
- Nine RULE or SYSTEM records have no hypothesis line; five of them are bug fixes.
- Thirteen "held for a human reading" items sit in `STATE.md` with no ID, default or date.

## Decision
1. **Decided for you.** D-009 stands: the scheduler decides and keeps moving. Added to it: a decision in an owner class of `Harness/H08 §2` (a pillar, a shape constant, what the player feels or is promised, a scope valve, a go or no-go at a gate) also gets one line in `OPEN.md` under the standing item "Decided for you", with what it changes. It never blocks. The default is that it stands; the owner reverses a line by saying so. Lines leave at the next stage gate.
2. **Decisions live on `main`.** A decision file is committed on `main` first and carried forward. Numbers are taken from the "next free" line of `STATE.md` on `main` and from nowhere else. At the next carry the scheduler copies to `main` the records that live only on later branches (D-020, D-022 to D-024, D-028, D-032 to D-035, D-037 to D-040, D-042 to D-044), and `INDEX.md` says which branch holds a record's work.
3. **Tags of `number_change_log.csv`**, as the log has used them: `[M]` read by a tool on this build (a bot battery, a probe, a rendered frame), a bound and never a reading from people; `[S]` a value taken from the handoff; `[G]` a first value with no source (`[H]` in older rows means the same, and new rows use `[G]`); `[P]` a reading from people, a validator session or an owner run. No old row is retagged. `Harness/H07 §6` is read through this item in this project.
4. **Hypothesis lines.** From here on a RULE or SYSTEM record carries a hypothesis line in the form of `Harness/H08 §5`, with its measure and its revert, or it is sent back. A fix of behaviour that differs from the spec is recorded as BUG and needs none. Earlier records are not rewritten.
5. **Held items leave prose.** Each item `STATE.md` holds "for a human reading" becomes a job, a decision, or a line in `OPEN.md`.

## Hypothesis
We believe one standing digest moves the owner's sight of owner-class calls from clauses in a state file to one list the owner can answer in a minute, because that is the only part of the window D-009 needed to keep; measured by whether any line is reversed, or read at all, by the S1 gate; reverted to D-009 as written if the owner says the list is noise.

# D-081 — A boss-fight clause is judged on the damage the boss takes; a fight's length is never read alone

- **Date:** 2026-11-13
- **Decider:** scheduler (delegated, D-009), on the Mechanics module session's Draft 18 (`Validation/boss_fight_measure_plan.md`, `plan/boss-fight-measure` ce384a6)
- **Class:** METHOD (how a reading is judged), with a printed block in a tool and two fields in a run record. No rule of play and no number
- **Status:** accepted; the tool's block before the S2 exit battery, the fields with D-073's branch, on `dev/s2`

## Context
- D-072, D-074 and D-079 each judge an evolution in "the boss fight" by a window of `Tools/evolve_read.py`: every source's damage a minute to everything alive between the boss's start and its end, rule against hold, as the median over the runs that reach the boss.
- Reading D-079, the Mechanics session reported that the evolved Blade Halo kills the boss more slowly than the hold, because its fight lasts 128 s against 93 s at a window of 1.03, and that the window could not see it. The scheduler passed that to the owner. It was wrong, and the session corrected it from the boss's own records before anything was recorded.
- The Warden's health is not fixed. `boss.md` R-BOS-09 sets its effective max once, from the damage it takes in the first eight seconds times the target length of the fight, clamped between 9 600 and 64 000. A build that hits the boss harder at the start meets a boss with more health. In D-079's Tempest pair the hold's fights sit on the floor in 8 of 15 runs and the rule's in 3 of 13, with a median boss of 14 871: the rule's fight is longer while it deals more to the boss (1.04 of the hold by the median, 1.15 pooled).
- Read again from the boss's own records, rule against hold, damage dealt to the boss a minute, median then pooled, beside the window as judged: D-072 0.99 and 0.91 (window 0.91, bar 0.6); D-074 1.07 and 1.13 (1.12, bar 0.9), its second pair 1.17 and 1.16; D-079 1.04 and 1.15 (1.03, bar 0.9), its second pair 1.35 and 1.37. No landed rule would have missed. The window and the boss's own measure agree within 0.03 pooled and 0.08 by the median, and the boss takes 0.86 to 0.96 of the fight's damage on every rule and hold arm.

## Options
- **Judge on the damage the boss takes** (the draft): exact, from the boss's own health, and not moved by what else is alive.
- **Keep the window**: it agreed five times out of five, and it is still not what the clause means.
- **Judge on the fight's length**: it measures the calibration, not the build.

## Decision
The draft, as written.

1. From the next rule on, a boss-fight clause is judged on the damage dealt to the boss a minute (its effective max times the share taken, over the fight's seconds), as the median over the runs that reached the boss, rule against hold on the same base and seeds; ten runs must reach it. The pooled figure is printed beside the median, since the two part where some runs sit on the health floor.
2. The old window stays as a printed line, so that the landed rules can be compared with what follows.
3. A fight's length is never printed or cited alone: the median effective max and the count of runs on a clamp stand beside it, and a reading that names a longer fight says which case it is (the build's damage fell after its opening, or the boss sat on the floor).
4. Printed with every fight: the old window; the length with the effective max and the clamp count; the damage the hero takes a minute in the fight; the boss's health left in the fights that were lost.
5. The three landed rules are not judged again. Their Results point here.
6. An evolution's own strikes are counted apart from its weapon's rounds by two fields on the run file's `weapon` record, `strike_hits` and `strike_damage`, from the impacts D-074 already marks. Not a second source id: a source is what a kill is credited to, and it would move a Discovery counter, the Keeper share of the power split and the dash refund. Telemetry only; no hash moves. They ride with the band's equipment branch and its whole gate (D-082 item 9; first placed with D-073's branch).
7. A rule for every reading that crosses arms, not only this one: where the director or the boss scales a thing by the player's own output, as R-BOS-09 does, nothing downstream of it (a length, damage taken, health left) is compared across arms without the scaled value beside it.
8. The window BEFORE the boss has a ceiling of its own, and it is supply. By `director.md` R-DIR-10 the director scales enemy health and damage by run time only, and nothing but the Warden's health is set by the player's output. But R-DIR-05's spawn credit is spent without a spawn while the field is at its alive cap, so a build that kills fast is fed the timeline's full rate and no more: damage a minute before the boss is bounded by the health the timeline supplies. A window before the boss near 1.0 can mean "as strong as the hold" or "both at the ceiling", and no evolution can read 1.3 against a hold that is already there. So a window before the boss is never read as power without saying whether the hold is supply-bound (kills a minute against spawns a minute). This bears on `REFORGE.POWER_MIN` and on every reading so far that found an evolution near 1.0; it is read for the ten evolutions from the run files that exist, for the record on what an evolution must give.
9. A sentence about the boss goes into a record or to the owner only after the boss's own records have been read. The scheduler passed on an interim reading as a finding; it corrected that to the owner the same hour.

## Hypothesis
None: a method. It is kept if the record on what an evolution must give, after D-073, can be written for all ten evolutions from these lines without a reading being argued about; it is reopened if a clause judged on it and the old window ever disagree on a verdict.

## The supply reading (the Mechanics session, 2026-11-13, from run files that exist)
Item 8 was read the same night from the director's own records between the pick and the boss, for the holds of all ten evolutions (the battery of `S2_evolutions.md`) and for the three pairs of that night.
- Every hold is supply-bound, and so is every evolved arm. Kills over spawns before the boss read 0.99 to 1.00 on all ten holds (333 to 358 spawns a minute), 0.97 on the night's clean builds, and 0.96 to 0.99 even on the ports that lose the run. The field never holds more than a quarter of its alive cap, usually under a tenth, and no spawn credit is ever spent without a spawn. So before the boss every arm kills what the timeline sends at the rate it sends it: that window measures the timeline. `reforging.md` says in its own open lines that the power band is unread and may be a field cap; it is one, on all ten.
- The hold is not the band's control either. By the bot's rule the hold takes its first Path card at the Reforging hand, so "about 1.0 of its hold" says an evolution is worth about what the Path's own pick is worth. It never compared a pick with no pick, which is what `REFORGE.POWER_MIN` speaks of. No battery has an arm that declines the hand.
- So the sentence of D-079's item 9, "every evolution read so far lands near 1.0, under `REFORGE.POWER_MIN`", is a finding about the measure and the control and not about the evolutions. Whether any of the ten reaches 1.3 of no pick is unread. What the window before the boss does show is a floor: an arm that falls under about 0.95 is failing to clear what is sent, and the ports that read 0.80 lose their runs.
- What can read the band, to be drafted before the S2 exit battery so that the battery reads it once: an arm that declines the Reforging hand (a switch of the bot, off by default), which gives the band its own denominator; the boss fight by item 1, which is not supply-bound, with the calibrated health beside it; and for power against a crowd the probe's fixed scene (a standing crowd that outlasts the window) read three ways: no pick, the Path's card, the evolution. A denser timeline for the reading alone would work and reads a fight the game does not have; it is ranked last.

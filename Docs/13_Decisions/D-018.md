# D-018 — Share caps bind only above a floor of alive enemies

- **Date:** 2026-10-31
- **Decider:** scheduler (delegated, D-009)
- **Class:** RULE
- **Status:** reverted (hypothesis not met at either value)

## Context
1. The alive share caps (`C.DIR.SHARE_MAX_*`) compare a role with the alive count, so a build that keeps the field small holds one Turret at 0.08. The nine Forge and Storm arms without Tide spawn a mean of 36 Turrets in 400–780 s where the timeline asks for about 155, and spawned XP stays at 0.79 of the full-pickup model (`S1_balance_pass2.md` §3 item 3, §8 item 4; `after_bot`, 15 seeds).
2. Raising the caps failed T-DIR-01 (`S1_balance_pass2.md` §6 round 1). That section reads the failure as the same small field holding no-pick and weak builds at 300–500 s. The telemetry says otherwise: in `after_bot` no-pick holds 179–300 alive from 280 to 540 s (p10–p90) and at least 97 from 130 s on, while the no-Tide strong arms hold 7–32. No-pick's field is large, and the caps bind there too: Turrets do not walk in to die, so no-pick's Turret share rises to 0.08 and stops. §4's "a build with a full field is not touched" is wrong; a raised cap let Turrets pile up in exactly the field the caps protect.
3. So the two fields are separable by alive count, which a raised cap could not do. Weak arms straddle the line: the Tide arms read p50 32–59 in 280–540 s, p10 18–27.

## Decision
A share cap binds only while alive enemies exceed `C.DIR.SHARE_FLOOR_ALIVE` (GATED(S1), a new constant). At or under the floor the regular draw follows the row's mix (R-DIR-05). The cap values do not change, and no other number moves. Swarm still has no cap; events and drop-ins are unchanged. The rule replaces the "an empty field excludes nothing" implementation call, which is the floor at zero.

First value 30: at or above the no-Tide strong arms' p90 at 280–540 s (19–32), under no-pick from about 70 s on, so a pile-up field keeps its caps. The floor limits the total, so a field that grows past it binds again.

## Hypothesis
Recorded in `number_change_log.csv` before measuring: the no-Tide strong arms' mean Turrets spawned in 400–780 s at least double (36 → ≥ 72); spawned XP / model above 0.85 (0.79) over those arms; T-DIR-01 stays in `C.DIR.NOPICK_DEATH_BAND_S`; T-OFF-15 no worse than 200/201. At most two floor values are tried; if neither meets it, the rule is reverted and this record says so.

The T-OFF-15 clause is the tight one: boss runs already read 28–30 decisions against an upper limit of 31, and one level near L25 costs about 7.5 % XP. A floor that restores the whole model would push the 30s over.

## Result
Not met; reverted (`Docs/Validation/S1_share_floor.md`). Second value 15, chosen after 30 missed because the readings (0.79 with no floor, 0.93 at 30) put 0.86 near it.

| Floor | Turrets 400–780 s | XP / model | T-DIR-01 | T-OFF-15 | Boss / wins |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| none | 35.8 | 0.789 | 588.6 s | 200/201 | 201 / 153 |
| 30 | 120.4 | 0.926 | 649.3 s | 95/142 | 142 / 109 |
| 15 | 72.3 | 0.8499 | 642.0 s | 194/201 | 201 / 171 |

At 15 the T-OFF-15 misses are six Storm runs at 32 decisions and one slow build. The rule, constant, test and spec text are removed. Context item 2 stands: the pass 2 reading of why the cap raise failed is wrong, and the two fields do separate by alive count.

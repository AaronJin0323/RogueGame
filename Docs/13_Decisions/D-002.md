# D-002 — S1 Keepers: Storm, Forge, Tide

- **Date:** 2026-10-28
- **Decider:** scheduler (owner delegated: "do whatever you want, you decide")
- **Class:** SYSTEM
- **Status:** applied

## Context
OPEN O-002. S1 builds the complete identity layer (flags, 3 archetypes, enchantment, tiers, Intercession) for three Keepers; the other three follow in S2.

## Options
- A — Storm (bullet-hell), Forge (melee/burn), Tide (control)
- B — any trio including Shade, Radiance or Quicksilver

## Decision
A. The three specialities are the most distinct (range/density, close range/damage over time, control), so a validator can tell them apart and name their build — an S1 exit criterion.

## Hypothesis
We believe this trio lets ≥ 2 of 3 S1 validators name their archetype in their own words, because the three change weapon behaviour along unrelated axes; measured at the S1 validation sessions.

## Impact (dependency sweep)
- `02_Systems/keepers.md` expression tables and `09_Catalog/expression.csv` for Storm, Forge, Tide first

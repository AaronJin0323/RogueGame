# D-047 — Keeper share is the power a build loses without its blessings

- **Date:** 2026-11-11
- **Decider:** scheduler (delegated, D-009; the owner on 2026-11-11: "make the best decision for the game and fix docs, then tell development agents")
- **Class:** RULE
- **Status:** open (decided; applied on `main` by the change job that cites it, spec and tools in one commit)

## Context
`Validation/plans_audit.md` 3.2 and 5.3. Pillar 3 says at least `C.POWER.KEEPER_SHARE_L16` of effective power comes from Keepers at level sixteen. Three instruments claim to read it.

- `keepers.md` R-KEE-18 cites attribution (`Tools/power_split.py`) and a test id no spec ever defined.
- The test line of the same number reads ablation share (`Tools/probe.py`): the drop without blessings over the sum of four drops.
- `Validation/S1_kit.md` clause 4 uses attribution on session logs.
- On the same runs they disagree arm by arm (`Validation/S1_probes.md`): Forge/Furnace 0.791 by share and 0.45 by attribution; Storm/Downpour 0.461 and 0.59.
- Share cannot pass Storm: its denominator holds the drops of weapon levels and extra weapons, and a flag that multiplies a weapon's hits raises those drops with the Keeper's own power (`Validation/S1_keeper_power.md` §6). The previous project's own note says the same of its method: "interactions are spread pro rata".
- The probe already prints a third column, `loss`: one minus DPS without blessings over full DPS. On the nine arms it reads 0.572 to 0.865, mean 0.755; eight are at or above 0.60 and Tempest is the one below.
- `power_split.py` ends in `return 0  # stub`.

## Options
- A — Keep share. Storm fails whatever its numbers are.
- B — Share for adding arms, loss for multiplying arms. Two instruments and a classification to argue over.
- C — Loss for every arm.
- D — Attribution. The source calls it "indicative only"; D-015 credits a converted weapon's whole output to the Keeper, and a multiplier is undercounted.

## Decision
C.

- R-KEE-18, first clause: for a steered build at `C.TEL.POWER_CHECK_LEVEL`, removing every blessing costs at least `C.POWER.KEEPER_SHARE_L16` of its crowd DPS (`loss`, `Tools/probe.py`, method in `10_Tuning.md §3`). Third clause: the archetypes' losses sit within ±`C.POWER.ARCHETYPE_BAND` of their mean. The package-gain clause is unchanged.
- The test line of R-KEE-18 judges loss; `probe.py --check` judges loss and prints share beside it.
- The undefined test id leaves R-KEE-18 and the dependents of both constants.
- `power_split.py` stays the attribution view of one session log. It is printed beside a reading and never judged, and its stub line goes.
- Kit clause 4 is the probe's reading on the session's build hash (D-048).

Why loss: it is the pillar's own sentence, read as a counterfactual. It needs no partition, so a Keeper is not marked down for making the weapon matter. The package-gain band is its guard: a package inside 2.0 to 2.4 times costs at most 0.58 of DPS when removed, so the second Keeper's cards must carry the rest, which is what the level-eight hand is for. The two are read together.

## Hypothesis
We believe reading share as loss moves the share clause from unpassable for Storm (0.39 to 0.46 whatever the numbers) to a reading that names a weak arm (Tempest 0.572, eight of nine at or above the floor on the table in `S1_probes.md`), because the share's denominator rises with a multiplying Keeper's own power; measured by rereading the nine arms on `main` after D-045 and D-046 land, with the no-second-Keeper control `S1_probes.md` §7 item 2 asks for; reverted if the reread shows loss at or above the floor on every arm whatever blessings are held, since an instrument that cannot fail is no instrument.

# D-038 — A dash volley acts once on every weapon, each by its own shape; only Singularity's merge keeps the weapon's rule

- **Date:** 2026-11-10
- **Decider:** scheduler (delegated, D-009)
- **Class:** RULE
- **Status:** applied on `dev/s2` (`fix/dash-volley-rule`); bot readings only, no human reading

## Context
`keepers.md` R-KEE-34 says a Quicksilver dash start "fires every weapon once", at `dmg_mult`, as Keeper damage, with a dash refund per kill. The Vector Key says it to the player: "Dashing fires every weapon along the dash at ×1.5. Kills refund 0.15 s of dash." / "冲刺时所有武器沿冲刺路线齐射，伤害 ×1.5。击杀返还 0.15 秒冲刺冷却。" Slipstream Drive and the Vector layer grant the same flag.

`Validation/S2_dash_volley.md` closed the two weapons that dropped the volley's source (Orbital Drones, Sweep Beam) and left four evolved weapons whose own rule gave a dash volley nothing or folded it in. Read from the code at e40943c and from four tests written before any change (`Dusktracer.Keepers.S2.Card.Quicksilver.Vector.BladeHalo`, `.Cyclone`, `.PulseWall`, `.Singularity`):

| Weapon | Its rule | What a dash volley did | How often |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blade Halo (Orbital Drones evolved) | R-RFG-07: blades, no bolts | nothing | always |
| Cyclone (Arc Blade evolved) | R-RFG-10: sweeps only while the hero moves faster than `move_min_uu_s` | nothing when the hero's speed in the landing step is below the gate | whenever the player lets go of the stick at the landing |
| Pulse Wall (Ring Emitter evolved) | R-RFG-12: one ring per weapon | nothing while a ring lives | in effect always: the weapon's cooldown is the ring's life, so a ring follows a ring (T-RFG-12 `PulseWallGap`) |
| Singularity (Gravity Mine evolved) | R-RFG-14: a mine placed while one lives adds its damage to it | its damage, at `dmg_mult`, joined the live mine; the blast kept the live mine's source, so no refund | whenever a mine lives |

The source (`Handoff_Dusktracer/game/reference/specs/keeper_identity_spec.md`) gives `VolleyOnDash` the weapons `All` with one fallback, Melee's sweep at the landing, and names "Arc Blade → Cyclone" with Vector as a build ("a dash-cleaving whirlwind"). It names no weapon a dash volley skips.

## Options
- **A. The weapon rules stand, and the card says so.** No code. The Key's text has no room for four exceptions (90 characters en, 40 zh, R-KEE-08), and a card that does nothing on the weapon the player just evolved is a wasted pick the game never shows.
- **B. R-KEE-34 wins everywhere, literally.** A second Pulse Wall ring and a second Singularity mine. That breaks "one ring per weapon" and "all your mines merge into one", the two sentences those evolutions are, and doubles Pulse Wall, whose strength is held for a human reading (D-020).
- **C. "Once" holds for every weapon, in that weapon's own shape.** Each weapon keeps what it is; the dash volley is one act of it, the volley's source and multiplier kept wherever the act is a new emission.

## Decision
C, per weapon. No catalog number is added or moved: every case reads `dmg_mult` and the weapon's own row.

1. **Blade Halo: R-KEE-34 applies.** A dash volley is every blade striking once where it is (`contact_r_uu`, the row's damage × `dmg_mult`), as `Keeper.Quicksilver`, refund included; no blade's turn is spent. Why: before this the Key did nothing at all on the weapon; "no bolts" is kept; the drones' dash volley is already "every drone once"; a blade's strike is the weapon's act as Arc Blade's sweep is, and the card already calls that "fires".
2. **Cyclone: R-KEE-34 applies.** The landing sweep of a dash volley does not ask the hero's speed in that step. Why: a dash is movement, so "Standing still it stops" / "站定时停转" is not contradicted; the source names Vector with Cyclone as a build; and whether the Key works should not hang on a stick still being held one step after a dash. The weapon's own turning keeps its gate.
3. **Pulse Wall: R-KEE-34 applies, inside "one ring".** A dash volley starts the live ring again at the hero, and from then the ring is the dash volley's: `Keeper.Quicksilver`, `dps` × `Damage` × `dmg_mult`, refund per kill. With no ring live it opens one, as before. Why: a ring always lives, so the weapon rule made the Key dead on this weapon; a second ring was rejected (Option B); and R-RFG-08 already settled the same case for Sweep Beam, one beam per weapon and a free volley starts it again as its own. The weapon's own next ring follows when the dash volley's ring ends, so ring time is not added.
4. **Singularity: the weapon rule stands.** A dash volley's mine joins the live mine at `dmg_mult` and the mine keeps the source of the volley that laid it. Why: nothing is wasted, the damage arrives at the Key's multiplier; merging is the card ("All your mines merge into one" / "所有引力雷合成一颗"), and both cards stay true in both languages; a mine is one emission with one source (R-WPN-07), and the rule is symmetric, since a mine the dash volley laid is Quicksilver's whatever the weapon adds to it, refund and all. Splitting the blast by share would strike twice and run every on-hit flag twice, a power change; relabelling the whole mine would give Quicksilver the weapon's own damage. What is given up: the refund for kills of a mine the weapon laid first.

Chain Trigger's free volley is not a dash volley and is not changed: on Blade Halo it still strikes nothing, on Pulse Wall it still opens no ring while one lives, and Cyclone at rest still ignores it. Decided since by D-039.

Card text: none changes. With this call the Vector Key, the layer line, Slipstream Drive and the keyword note are true of every weapon in en and zh-Hans, so no string moved and no loc file was rebuilt.

## Hypothesis
We believe letting a dash volley act on Blade Halo, Cyclone and Pulse Wall moves the count of weapon forms on which a Vector Key pick can do nothing from 3 of 20 to 0, because the four tests read it so before the change; measured by T-KEE-34 now and by the first human session that holds a Vector card with one of the three; reverted for Pulse Wall alone if that session reads the restarted ring as losing the wall the player was standing behind.

## Result
- Before the change, on e40943c with only the tests added: `BladeHalo`, `Cyclone` and `PulseWall` fail, `Singularity` passes (it pins the rule that stands). After it, all ten `Vector.*` tests pass.
- Code, RULE class, sim layer, `DTWeaponSim.cpp` and `.h` only: `StepDrones`' Blade Halo branch strikes with every blade on a dash volley; `FireVolley`'s Cyclone gate skips a dash volley; `OpenForceField` takes `bRestart`, which a dash volley's Pulse Wall passes. It replaces the three early returns that dropped the volley; nothing is stacked beside them.

Its dash volleys now hit, at ×`dmg_mult`, and their kills refund the dash, so its inputs change. Nothing else can move: every new branch sits behind the dash volley's own switch.

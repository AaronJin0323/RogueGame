# D-039 — Chain Trigger's free volley acts on every weapon and never starts a live beam or ring again

- **Date:** 2026-11-10
- **Decider:** scheduler (delegated, D-009)
- **Class:** RULE
- **Status:** applied on `fix/chain-trigger-rule`, cut from `dev/s2` at 6a5afe3; test and harness readings only, no bot battery, no human reading

## Context
`Mod.ChainTrigger` says to the player: "A kill by this weapon fires one free volley." / "这把武器击杀敌人时，免费再齐射一次。" Its row has one number, `min_interval_s` (0.2), and no rule of its own in `modifiers.md`. It requires no cap and blocks none, so `matrix.csv` reads `yes` for all ten weapons, and an evolution's caps (R-RFG-04) never make it dormant: the socket takes it, the offer pool shows it and R-MOD-08 has nothing to say on all twenty weapon forms (`Dusktracer.Modifiers.ChainTrigger.EveryWeapon` reads that). The matrix has no way to refuse one evolution: every cap Blade Halo, Sweep Beam or Pulse Wall declares is shared with a form where the modifier works.

D-038 made a dash volley act on every weapon and left Chain Trigger's free volley as it was. Read from the code at 6a5afe3 and from seven tests written before any change (`Dusktracer.Modifiers.ChainTrigger.*`, run on 6a5afe3 with only the tests added). `.March` is thirty seconds of a crowd that walks in and dies, six enemies a second from 750 uu at 100 uu/s, at two healths, the weapon at level 8; it counts kills without and with the modifier.

| Weapon form | What a kill's free volley did | Kills, without → with (60 hp; 240 hp) | How often dead |
|---|---|---|---|
| Railstorm, Flechette, Carpet, Murmuration, Vortex | one more volley of rounds or shells, as `Modifier.ChainTrigger` | more in all ten readings | never |
| Cyclone (Arc Blade evolved) | one more sweep while the hero moves; nothing at rest (R-RFG-10) | 152 → 155; 134 → 143, moving | only at rest, where the weapon itself is silent and has no kill to trigger on |
| Singularity (Gravity Mine evolved) | its mines' damage joins the live mine, which keeps its source (R-RFG-14); with none live it lays one as the modifier's | 162 → 168; 161 → 170 | never |
| Blade Halo (Orbital Drones evolved) | nothing: no bolts, and no strike (R-RFG-07) | 144 → 144; 0 → 0 | always |
| Pulse Wall (Ring Emitter evolved) | nothing while a ring lives (R-RFG-12), and the cooldown is the ring's life | 150 → 150; 75 → 75 | in effect always |
| Sweep Beam (Rail Lance evolved) | started the live beam again from its edge, as the modifier's (R-RFG-08) | 164 → 116; 114 → 69 | worse than dead: every kill sends the beam back over ground it has cleared |

So two sockets do nothing the game never shows, and a third costs kills. Sweep Beam was not on D-038's list: the test found it.

## Options
- **A. The weapon rules stand; the pair is not offered, or the card says so.** Needs a way to say it. The matrix cannot (above); a new cap or a per-evolution block list is a SYSTEM change and a catalog column; the card's one line serves every weapon. It would also leave Sweep Beam's restart, which is the worst of the three.
- **B. D-038 and R-RFG-08 applied as they stand: a free volley starts the live ring or beam again.** Measured, not argued. A dash is one press per cooldown; a kill comes as often as every `min_interval_s`. R-RFG-08 already does this to Sweep Beam and it loses 29 % and 39 % of its kills. Pulse Wall with the same branch (one line, built, run, put back): 150 → 70 and 75 → 16 kills, and enemy-steps inside 150 uu of the hero go from 0 to 45572. The wall the card promises is gone.
- **C. Each weapon in its own shape, and a kill never restarts what lives.** Where the volley is rounds, shells, mines or a sweep it is unchanged. Where the weapon is blades it is every blade once (D-038's shape). Where one beam or one ring lives per weapon, the kill's volley is one tick of it and the live one is left alone.
- **D. Hold all three for a human.** Leaves a pick that does nothing and one that hurts, with no number waiting on anyone: none of C's cases needs one.

## Decision
C. No catalog number is added or moved: every case reads the weapon's own row. A kill's free volley carries no multiplier.

1. **Blade Halo: the free volley acts.** Every blade strikes once where it is (`contact_r_uu`, the row's damage), as `Modifier.ChainTrigger`; no blade's turn is spent. Why: the same act D-038 gave a dash volley, and the same size as the plain weapon's, where a free volley is every drone once. "No bolts" is kept.
2. **Pulse Wall: the free volley acts, inside "one ring", without a restart.** While a ring lives, it strikes its band once more where it stands, for one tick of the row (`dps` × `Damage` × `tick_s`), as `Modifier.ChainTrigger`. The ring's place, size, clock, own ticks and source are untouched. With no ring live it opens one, as before, and that ring is the modifier's. Why not D-038's restart: Option B's reading. Why a tick: it is the ring's one act, as a touch is a blade's. At most one strike per `min_interval_s` against the ring's own tick each `tick_s`, so the modifier at most a little more than doubles the ring's ticks in a crowd that dies that fast, less than a second volley is to any other weapon.
3. **Sweep Beam: the free volley acts, inside "one beam", without a restart.** While a beam lives, the free volley is one tick of the beam (`width_deg`, `reach_uu`, the weapon's tick damage) along its own aim, the densest line (R-WPN-15), as `Modifier.ChainTrigger`; the live beam sweeps on. With no beam live it starts one, as before. This replaces the Chain Trigger half of R-RFG-08's "a free volley starts it again"; a dash volley still does (D-038). Why the volley's aim and not where the beam points: tried first, and against enemies one tick kills it struck an arc the beam had just emptied, 164 → 164 kills with no hit of the modifier's. Every volley of this weapon is aimed at the densest line; so is this one.
4. **Cyclone: the weapon rule stands.** At rest a kill's free volley does not sweep. Why: "Standing still it stops" / "站定时停转" is the card; D-038 let a dash through because a dash is movement, and a kill is not. Nothing is lost that the game hides: at rest the weapon makes no kill of its own to trigger on, and moving the free sweep lands.
5. **Singularity: the weapon rule stands.** As D-038: the mine is one emission and keeps the source of the volley that laid it, so the free volley's damage joins at full size and reads as the weapon's, and a mine the free volley laid is the modifier's whatever joins it. Attribution only: no rule reads `Modifier.ChainTrigger` from a hit or a kill (`Validation/S2_dash_volley.md`).
6. **The other five evolutions and the ten plain weapons: unchanged.**

A dash volley is not touched: D-038 stands whole. The code tells the two apart by the dash volley's own switch. (Since then D-040 has given the dash volley this shape on Pulse Wall and Sweep Beam.)

Card text: none changes. On seventeen forms the line is literally true, on Singularity the volley arrives inside the one mine, and a resting Cyclone is its own card's exception. On Pulse Wall and Sweep Beam with one live, a kill gets one tick of a volley, not a whole ring or beam: "one free volley" says more than the weapon gives. A whole second ring or beam is what R-RFG-12 and R-RFG-08 forbid, and a restart is Option B. The line serves every weapon and there is no per-weapon line for a modifier, so the gap is recorded here and left for a person to read.

## Hypothesis
We believe letting a kill's free volley strike with Blade Halo's blades, tick Pulse Wall's live ring and tick Sweep Beam along its aim, with no restart, moves the count of weapon forms on which a Chain Trigger socket adds nothing or costs kills from 3 of 20 to 0, because `.EveryWeapon` and `.March` read it so before and after; measured by T-MOD-16 now and by the first bot battery or human session that holds the modifier on one of the three; reverted for Pulse Wall alone if a reading puts a Pulse Wall build with the modifier above `C.REFORGE.POWER_MAX`, and for Sweep Beam alone if a person reads the tick on the densest line as a second beam.

## Result
- Before the change, on 6a5afe3 with only the tests added: 5 of 7 fail on 9 lines. `.BladeHalo` (no strike), `.PulseWall` (no strike, 2 lines), `.SweepBeam` (2 hits of the modifier's for 1, and the weapon's own sweep cut from 2 hits to 0), `.EveryWeapon` (Blade Halo and Pulse Wall add no damage), `.March` (Sweep Beam kills fewer at both healths). `.Cyclone` and `.Singularity` pass: they pin the rules that stand. After it all seven pass.
- `.March` after, kills without → with: Blade Halo 144 → 145 and 0 → 0, with enemy-steps inside 150 uu 1137 → 207 at 60 hp; Pulse Wall 150 → 157 and 75 → 120; Sweep Beam 164 → 171 and 114 → 126. The other seven evolved forms and the ten plain ones read as before.
- Code, RULE class, sim layer, `DTWeaponSim.cpp` and `.h` only: `StepDrones`' Blade Halo branch strikes on any free volley, at the dash multiplier only for a dash; `FireVolley` names a free volley that is not a dash volley, and for it Sweep Beam emits one arc and Pulse Wall calls the new `StrikeForceField`. It replaces Blade Halo's and Pulse Wall's early returns and Sweep Beam's restart for this volley; nothing is stacked beside them.

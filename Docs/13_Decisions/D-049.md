# D-049 — The S3 exit passes on the designer's two of three; fresh means a fresh save

- **Date:** 2026-11-11
- **Decider:** scheduler (delegated, D-009; the owner on 2026-11-11: "make the best decision for the game and fix docs, then tell development agents")
- **Class:** GO-NOGO
- **Status:** applied on `main` in `12_Production §2` (this commit). It replaces D-034, which lives on `dev/s3`; the carry edits `Validation/findings_S3.md`, `Validation/S3_kit.md`, the reason text in `Tools/s3_exit.py` and D-034's status there

## Context
`Validation/plans_audit.md` 3.3 and 5.9.

- `12_Production §2` gives the S3 exit as "3 fresh validators × 3 runs, pass 2 of 3". D-034 read that as two mandatory rows, each needing two of three validators, with the owner's row left out, and said the docs list no criteria.
- They do. The designer's rule of 2026-10-19: "continue to the full game if 2 of 3 hold — ≥ 2 of 3 testers ask for 'one more run' unprompted; ≥ 2 testers can name their 流派; the designer still wants to play after the playtest. If all testers say it felt tiring, or none can name a 流派, fix the core feel before adding content" (`Handoff_Dusktracer/game/reference/original_docs/03_Roadmap.md`, the v0.1 week). The blueprint carries it as "外部试玩（3 人、新存档、3 局）2/3 标准" (`Handoff_Dusktracer/game/G02_Blueprint_DocSet_zh.md`, the S3 row).
- The blueprint's "新存档" is a new save. `12_Production` turned it into "fresh validators" and the S3 kit into people who have never played any build, which doubles the people the project needs.
- On a fresh save every Amp and Trait is shut, so "name your build" is asked on the thinnest pool the game has (`Validation/S3_tree.md`).

## Options
- A — Keep D-034.
- B — Restore the designer's rule: the exit passes when two of three hold, and the designer's stop line stands beside it.
- C — B with every band added. The bands are S1's to lock (D-048).

## Decision
B.

- The S3 exit passes when at least two of these three hold at the external session: at least two of three validators ask for another run unprompted; at least two of three name their build in their own words; the owner still wants to play after it.
- The stop line: if all three validators call it tiring, or none can name a build, the core is fixed before any content is added, whatever else held.
- "Fresh" is a fresh save. A validator who played the S1 or S2 build may return; the sheet records what they have played. Three people can serve every exit.
- The bands and tool rows are read and reported in `findings_S3.md` and are not part of this rule, as in D-034.
- The pass rule is still read by a person from the filled file, never from the logs.

Not decided here: whether a fresh save should open one archetype's Amps per S1 Keeper. The scheduler decides it under D-009 before the S3 kit is built, with the reading in `S3_tree.md` in front of it.

## Hypothesis
We believe restoring the designer's rule moves the S3 exit from a rule that can fail on a row the first-run pool cannot express to the rule its owner wrote, and halves the people needed, because D-034 was written without the two sources above; measured at the external S3 session; reversed only by the owner.

# D-055 — Modules are developed side by side by module sessions under the scheduler; audio opens with a first wave

- **Date:** 2026-11-11
- **Decider:** the owner, in the scheduler's session on 2026-11-11 (three messages, quoted below); the detail is the scheduler's (delegated, D-009)
- **Class:** PROCESS, GO-NOGO. It amends D-050 item 1 and D-043's hold on audio
- **Status:** applied (this commit: `AGENTS.md`, `12_Production §1`, `STATE.md`, `OPEN.md`); the three sessions are started the same day

## Context
Until today one scheduler session ran every job through its own subagents, one or two engine jobs at a time. The owner asked for more, in these words:

- "feel free fanning out subagents for improve effectiveness"
- "different modules may be developed in parallel, such as game mech, visual art, sound effectsm, etc. As long as there is no conflict, create subsessions for each modules, all controlled by Central Project Scheduler, and each subsession may fan out their own subagents for developing."
- "每个决策、设计和计划session都应该使用Fable 5.2 Extra，负责开发的子agent则使用Opus 5.5 Medium" (every decision, design and planning session uses Fable 5.2 at extra-high effort; the subagents that develop use Opus 5.5 at medium effort)

What stands in the way of doing that blindly:

- Every job builds and runs on one PC. Its CPU faults on two cores (D-052), and three kinds of reading must run alone (D-050 item 4). Nothing in the Tools stops two sessions from starting four builds at once.
- D-050 item 1 holds everything but S4 wave 1 until people have played, and D-043 holds all audio "until audio decisions". The owner names sound effects as a module, and no audio exists (`Validation/S4_artbook_gaps.md` on `dev/s4`: "No sound path in Source").
- The session A package is built from `main` at e4926d4. A change to `Source`, `Content` or `Config` on `main` makes it stale (`Tools/PadCheck.ps1`).
- Hit and kill feedback (wave 1 package E) and hit and kill sounds both need the same read-only tap in the hit resolve phase (D-043, Unverified).
- The account's Fable limit stopped the scheduler for five hours on 2026-11-11.

## Options
- A — Keep one session and only add subagents. It ignores the owner's second message.
- B — One session per module, each free to merge its own work. Fast, and two mergers on one branch is the failure the handover of 2026-11-11 removed.
- C — One session per module that plans, designs and reviews its module and fans out its own developers; the scheduler alone merges, numbers decisions, takes the alone readings and holds the machine rules.

## Decision
C, in nine items.

1. **Three module sessions**, each started by the scheduler and answering to it:
   - **Mechanics** (the owner's "game mech"): the sim and its rules. `Source/Dusktracer/` except `Presentation` and `Audio`; the system specs in `02_Systems/` except the presentation and audio specs; the gameplay catalogs; the bot, probe and battery tools. It works on `main`, `dev/s2` and `dev/s3`, each change on the lowest branch that has the defect (D-050 item 3).
   - **Visual**: S4 Presentation that is seen. `Source/Dusktracer/Presentation`, `SourceArt/`, `Content/DT/Materials`, `Meshes` and `Maps`, `Tools/blender/`, `Tools/Scenes/`, the presentation specs, the palette. It works on `dev/s4`.
   - **Sound**: S4 Presentation that is heard. A new `Source/Dusktracer/Audio/`, the generator and import tools under `Tools/audio/`, generated sources, `Content/DT/Audio/`, a new `02_Systems/audio.md`, the Settings volume reader. It works on `dev/s4`.
   A fourth module is added by a record like this one when work appears that fits none of the three.
2. **Models, by the owner's word.** A module session decides, designs and plans, so it runs on Fable 5.2 at extra-high effort, as the scheduler does. Every subagent that develops (code, tools, tests, data wiring, document edits to a plan) runs on Opus 5.5 at medium effort. Work judged by eye or by ear (a mesh, a material, a frame, a sound) is the session's own or a Fable 5.2 extra-high subagent's, one at a time. A session keeps its own context for judgement: reading widely, digesting logs and writing code go to Opus subagents.
3. **The scheduler alone merges** into `main`, `dev/s2`, `dev/s3` and `dev/s4`, and alone pushes. A module session and its subagents commit on job branches only (`s1/`, `s2/`, `s3/`, `s4/` and `plan/` prefixes), never touch the main checkout, and report a branch as ready with its base tip and its `GATE:` line. A branch is gated on top of the tip of its base as it stands when it is handed over; if the base moved, the session merges the base in and gates again. The scheduler reads the report and the diff, looks at frames itself, fast-forwards, pushes and carries forward.
4. **Decisions.** Inside its accepted plan a module session makes its own design choices and records them in its notes. Anything that changes a rule, a number, a scope or another module's files is a decision record: the session drafts it in the form of `13_Decisions/` inside a plan note on a `plan/` branch, as D-043 was drafted, and the scheduler accepts it, numbers it and lands it on `main` first (D-051), or returns it. The scheduler keeps `STATE.md`, `OPEN.md`, `INDEX.md`, `AGENTS.md` and the decision files.
5. **No conflict, by ownership.** A module edits its own files (item 1). Shared files follow the wave 1 rule (`Validation/S4_wave1.md` §2 item 9 on `dev/s4`): `constants.yaml`, `08_Constants.md`, `number_change_log.csv` and `09_Catalog/licenses.csv` take new rows only, each job in its own block; generated headers and `.locres` files are regenerated, never merged; `Tools/Gate.ps1`, `Smoke.ps1`, `Perf.ps1`, `Machine.ps1`, `HiddenLaunch.ps1` and `contrast_check.py` are changed only by a job the scheduler dispatches. A module that needs a file of another module asks the scheduler, who gives the job to the owning module or grants the one exception by name.
6. **The seam between sim and presentation.** Presentation and audio read the sim through read-only taps and never write sim state; a tap's first check is that no seed 9107 hash moves. The hit and kill tap is one job, done first by the Visual session as the first step of package E and merged before any sound is wired to it. Sound's first jobs (item 8) do not need it.
7. **One PC.** A heavy job is a compiler run, an engine or game launch (tests, smoke, bots, commandlets, a cook), a package, or a Blender render or bake.
   - A module session and all its subagents together run at most one heavy job at a time. The scheduler's own merges and carries take one more.
   - The scheduler takes the machine for an alone reading (D-050 item 4) by writing `<git common dir>\dt-locks\alone.txt`, which says who holds it and for what. While the file exists nobody else starts a heavy job; text work, git and small Python tools go on. Every session and subagent looks for the file before each heavy job.
   - A Tools job makes both rules a lock the scripts take themselves; until it lands they are held by the briefs.
   - A session reuses a built worktree for the next job on the same branch where it can, and names its finished worktrees in its reports so the scheduler can purge their `Intermediate` and `Binaries`.
8. **Audio opens with a first wave on `dev/s4`**, on the owner's word. This replaces D-043's "Until audio decisions: all audio" and is the one exception to D-050 item 1. The Sound session's first job is the audio decisions themselves, as a plan note with a decision draft (item 4): the runtime (the gap note names RogueHero's own MIT runtime as the port), sounds that are generated by a tool in this repo and never copied or downloaded (R-PRE-10, the licence log), the mix classes and the volume setting, the first wave's list and what it leaves out, how a sound is checked without a person (a count, a level, a length, a spectrum) and what only a person can judge. It builds nothing that the plan does not hold. Wave 1's four limits (D-043 Option C) bind audio too: no open human reading needed to build it, no sim number and no rule changed, no AI-sourced or downloaded asset, checked without a window.
9. **What does not change.** D-050 items 2 to 8 stand. Still held until session A has been held and read: a second visual wave, S5 and any later stage, new gameplay content, and any number tuned on a bot survival clause. `main` takes no change to `Source`, `Content` or `Config` before session A unless it removes a defect a validator would meet; Mechanics work for `main` that is not such a defect is prepared on its branch and waits. A module session may plan held work; it does not build it.

## Hypothesis
We believe three module sessions raise the gated job branches landed per day above the twelve that one session landed on `main` on 2026-11-11, without raising odd gate failures, because the modules own different files and each holds one heavy job at a time; measured by the merge log in the scheduler's records and by odd failures per gate (D-052 counts them); reverted to one session with subagents if two landings in a week need a conflict between modules resolved by hand, if odd failures pass one in ten gates, or if the Fable limit stops the scheduler twice in a week.

- Rules replaced: D-050 item 1 (audio excepted), D-043 "Until audio decisions: all audio". `12_Production §1` gains the module sessions.
- Files: `AGENTS.md` (a section), `12_Production.md`, `STATE.md`, `OPEN.md` (O-013 line), `INDEX.md`.

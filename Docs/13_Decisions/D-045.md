# D-045 — An arriving Rusher charges through where the hero stood

- **Date:** 2026-11-11
- **Decider:** scheduler (delegated, D-009; the owner on 2026-11-11: "make the best decision for the game and fix docs, then tell development agents")
- **Class:** RULE
- **Status:** applied 2026-11-11 on `s1/d045-d046` for `main` (Result below); the nineteen-arm SOURCES reading and session A are open, and the carry to `dev/s2`, `dev/s3` and `dev/s4` is the scheduler's

## Context
`Validation/plans_audit.md` 5.2. A drop-in Rusher spawns in WindUp where it lands (`enemies.md` R-ENM-13) and charges the role's fixed length: `C.ROLE.RUSHER_CHARGE_SPEED_UU_S` 1500 for `C.ROLE.RUSHER_CHARGE_S` 0.65 s, 975 uu. The director lands it on the threat ring, `C.DIR.THREAT_RING_MIN_UU` 1000 to `C.DIR.THREAT_RING_MAX_UU` 1200 from the hero. With both radii (40 + 40) the charge reaches 1055 uu, so about a quarter of landings can touch a hero who stands still, and none can touch one who has moved away. Rushers are 70 % of drop-ins (`timeline_arena1.csv`).

- The kill-horizon guarantee (R-DIR-09) counts each arrival as a threat action whether or not it can reach.
- `Validation/S1_balance_pass2.md` read the zero Rusher damage as the skill check working.
- The owner's run took ten hits and none from a Rusher (`Validation/S1_owner_run.md` §3).
- The source says what was meant: the charger "arrives 1 000–1 200 uu out with its charge line already drawn … then charges through where the hero stood" (`Handoff_Dusktracer/game/reference/original_docs/01_GameDesign.md` §3.5).

So the port kept the landing ring and gave the arrival the walking Rusher's length. The drop-in is the director's core duty between beats, and most of it cannot hit.

## Options
- A — Land Rusher arrivals inside the fixed charge's reach. Two new ring constants; the threat lands nearer than the ring the kill horizon was sized for.
- B — An arrival's charge runs from where it lands through the position its hero held when the wind-up began, and a little beyond. Speed, wind-up and telegraph lead are unchanged; the band shows the real length. This is the source's rule.
- C — Leave it and call arrivals area denial. The guarantee would then count threats that are not threats.

## Decision
B.

- `enemies.md` R-ENM-13, arrival clause: a Rusher that spawns in WindUp charges from its position through the point its hero held when the wind-up began and `C.ROLE.RUSHER_ARRIVAL_OVERSHOOT_UU` beyond it, at the role's charge speed, clipped by the arena as any charge is. Its band has that length and the Rusher's collision width, drawn for the whole wind-up. A Rusher that walked into trigger range keeps R-ENM-11's fixed length.
- New constant `C.ROLE.RUSHER_ARRIVAL_OVERSHOOT_UU`, GATED(S1), first value 200 uu, `[G]`: two and a half hero widths, so a step back along the line does not escape and a step off it does.
- The test of R-ENM-13 gains three assertions: for every landing point on the threat ring the band covers the hero's position at wind-up start; a hero who stands still is hit; a hero who moves one hero width off the line before the charge is not.
- The job checks `RusherStampede`: any Rusher that spawns in WindUp follows this clause.

RULE, not TUNE: no existing constant moves. The spec's own sentence gave arrivals the wrong length.

## Hypothesis
We believe charging through the hero's position moves the share of Rusher arrivals whose band covers that position from about a quarter (geometry above) to all of them, and Rusher-source damage in steered runs from none to some, because the charge is now as long as the distance it was landed at; measured by the new assertions and by `Tools/pressure.py` SOURCES on the nineteen arms before and after; reverted if the assertions cannot pass without shortening `C.ENEMIES.TELEGRAPH_LEAD_MIN_S`, or if two validators at session A (D-048) call an arrival a hit they had no time to answer.

## Result
Applied as decided, with no lead shortened (`C.ENEMIES.TELEGRAPH_LEAD_MIN_S` and the wind-up are unchanged).

- T-ENM-13 (`Dusktracer.Enemies.TelegraphLead`) holds the three assertions on 48 landings (16 angles at the ring's inner edge, middle and outer edge). Before the change the band covered the hero from 0 of 48 and a hero who stood still was hit from 16 (the inner edge only); after, 48 and 48. A fourth line checks the arena clip.
- One hero width off the line is exactly where the two circles touch (both radii are 40 uu), and R-ENM-14 reads a touch as no contact. The assertion holds exactly on the four axis landings, where the arithmetic is exact; on the other landings rounding decides a touch, so the test steps 0.01 uu further. No rule or number moved for this; it is recorded so that nobody reads "one hero width" as a margin.
- `RusherStampede`: its Rushers are not flagged `Arrival` (`DTDirectorCore.cpp`, only `StepDropIns` sets the flag). They walk into trigger range and keep R-ENM-11's fixed length, so the clause does not reach them.
- Not changed, and noted: an arrival killed in its wind-up resolves its band as a hazard that also hits at a touch (`<=`), where a live charge does not. The band's new length passes through that path unchanged (T-ENM-13's killed-arrival lines pass).
- Seed 9107 moved on all four arms read, each after the first drop-in (270 s): `Validation/S1_d045_d046.md`. The hypothesis' second half, Rusher-source damage on the nineteen arms by `Tools/pressure.py` SOURCES, is not read: this job ran no battery, and four single runs are not that reading.

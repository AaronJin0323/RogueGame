# D-053 — Until people have played, Hammer's Key changes nothing on Orbital Drones or the Gravity Mine and says so; Hard Blow does what its card says

- **Date:** 2026-11-11
- **Decider:** scheduler (delegated, D-009)
- **Class:** RULE (`weapons.md` R-WPN-08, two cells stated). The Hard Blow fix rides in the same job as a BUG and needs no hypothesis
- **Status:** decided 2026-11-11; the change job is `s1/forge-truth`

## Context
`Validation/S1_hammer_key.md` ("Needs a rule"), `weapons.md` Open, the evidence digest of 2026-11-11 read on `main` 6f09c50.

- Queue item 5 fixed the Key on the Arc Blade (8f65657): its sweeps take the Key's arc and reach, and the first `rounds` sweeps deal the Key's multiplier, which covers every sweep at every level. Left open: what the Key does on Orbital Drones and on the Gravity Mine, whether the blade's sweeps count as cleaves, and which multiplier the blade takes.
- The Key is `Flag.ConvertMelee` with `rounds`, `arc_deg`, `radius_uu` and `dmg_mult` (`09_Catalog/expression.csv`). With `C.WEAPON.CONVERT_MELEE_DAMAGE_RATIO` the card prints one multiplier, `{cleave_mult}`, and the sim deals that number (D-030).
- On the drones and the mine the Key alone moves nothing: it carries no `radius_scale`, and neither weapon fires a round to convert. The drone cell of R-WPN-08 is void.
- Every run starts with the Pulse Rifle (D-015), and the Key converts its rounds. So on `main` the Key never does nothing for a player; it adds nothing to the drones.
- A validator cannot hold the Gravity Mine: it is `starter=no` and enters a run only through `DT.Unlock`, which Shipping compiles out, and the session A kit is the Shipping package.
- Blade sweeps are not cleaves in the sim (`EmitArc(..., false)`). If they were, Hammer's Burn on a cleave and Masterwork would act on them and their damage would count as Forge's.
- The Forge/Hammer arm already reads the highest loss of the nine (0.937) and a package gain of 2.89 against a band of 2.0 to 2.4 (`Validation/S1_d047_reread.md`).
- Found beside it: Hard Blow's card says cleaves knock enemies back, and no cleave reads `knockback_uu`. Only Forge's Intercession ring reads it, whether or not the card is held. The owner took the card in the one human run on record.

## Options
- A — The Key acts through a weapon's own strike where no round converts: drone bolts and the mine's blast deal the Key's multiplier. Adds power to the strongest arm before anyone has calibrated the bands, and makes "rounds stop flying" false on the drones.
- B — The first drones stop and cleave. A new strike shape, which is new content (D-050 item 1).
- C — Unchanged on both weapons, stated in the rule's table and on the card's weapon box; the rule question goes to the S1 gate with a question in the kit.
- For the blade: count Key-shaped sweeps as cleaves now, or leave them the blade's and ask at the S1 gate.
- For the multiplier: the one printed number, or a larger one on the blade with a second number on the card.

## Decision
C, the sweeps stay the blade's, and the one printed multiplier stays.

1. **R-WPN-08, drone and mine cells:** Hammer's Key leaves Orbital Drones and the Gravity Mine unchanged. The card's weapon box says so in the words D-030 already uses for a weapon a card does not touch. If the box says anything else today, that is a BUG against D-030 and is fixed in the job.
2. **Arc Blade:** as 8f65657 left it. Its sweeps under the Key are the blade's damage, not cleaves, and take the printed multiplier.
3. **Hard Blow (BUG):** a cleave knocks the enemies it hits back by `knockback_uu` while the card is held, through the one knockback path the Intercession ring already uses. The ring keeps reading the catalog value as it does now.
4. **Two questions go to the S1 gate decision, with session A's answers:** whether sweeps the Key reshapes are cleaves (D-015 says Keeper-caused reshaping is Keeper damage, which argues yes), and whether a Key acts on a weapon with no round to convert through that weapon's own strike (D-038 on `dev/s2` took that line for the dash volley). `Validation/S1_kit.md` asks a validator who held the Key with the blade or the drones what they expected the card to do there.

Why not act now: both open rules add power to the arm that already reads highest, on a band no person has calibrated; a stated "unchanged" is honest and costs nothing to reverse; and the one thing here that lies to a player today is Hard Blow, which is fixed.

## Hypothesis
We believe stating "unchanged" moves the Key on the drones from a silent nothing to a stated one, because the card's weapon box already speaks per weapon (D-030); measured by the box line T-OFF-13 pins for the drones and by the kit question at session A; reverted to option A at the S1 gate if two of the validators who held the Key with the drones or the blade say the card seemed broken there.

# D-082 — The Reforging band is read on the probe's fixed field against no pick; play gives the floors

- **Date:** 2026-11-13
- **Decider:** scheduler (delegated, D-009), on the Mechanics module session's Draft 19 (`Validation/reforge_band_reading_plan.md`, `plan/reforge-band-reading` 29dc11b) and D-081's supply reading
- **Class:** METHOD, with measuring equipment: a switch of the bot, one field of the probe's scene script, a stage of `Tools/probe.py`. One sentence of `reforging.md` R-RFG-18 changes, the one that says what the band is measured on. No rule of play and no number
- **Status:** the equipment is applied on `dev/s2` (0d2f1ca, 2026-11-13); the band is read as D-085 amends it; the S2 exit battery gains the band's step

## Context
- The source's sentence is the band: a steered build's damage after its level 16 pick rises 1.3 to 1.8 times, and the swarm's time to kill holds before and after. The port keeps it as `REFORGE.POWER_MIN` and `REFORGE.POWER_MAX` and read it once, damage a minute after the pick over before it in play: 1.00, "may be a field cap".
- D-081's supply reading: it is a field cap. Between the pick and the boss every arm of every battery kills what the timeline sends at the rate it sends it. And the carry batteries' hold takes its Path's card at that hand, so no reading has the band's denominator, the build without the pick.
- So nothing has read the band, for the six Path cards as little as for the ten evolutions. The carry batteries read a floor: an arm under about 0.95 is failing to clear what is sent.
- At the Reforging hand a player takes one card and cannot take nothing. "Over nothing" is the band's measure and never a player's choice; the choice a player has is between the Path's card and an evolution.

## Options
- **The probe's fixed field against no pick, with floors in play** (the draft).
- **A band on boss damage**: after the hand the arms are different runs with five minutes of different hands between the pick and the boss, and some cards are crowd tools by design.
- **A denser timeline for the reading alone**: it reads a fight the game does not have.
- **Leave the band unread** and keep judging evolutions against their holds: then no record can say what an evolution gives.

## Decision
The draft, as written, with one change to D-081.

1. The band is judged against a crowd, on the probe's fixed field. For each Reforging card: the builds that reach the hand (each run's level 16 snapshot), each read with the card and without it on the same field and seed. The card's lift is the damage in the window with it over the damage without it, as the median over its builds. The card is in band from `REFORGE.POWER_MIN` to `REFORGE.POWER_MAX`.
2. It is the source's own sentence, it changes one thing, and it cannot be saturated if the field is kept under its refill's ceiling, which the job proves for every scene before a figure is used.
3. One band, not two. The boss gets a floor.
4. The floors, in play: the carry battery with three arms on the same seeds (the card, the Path's card, no pick), judged against the no-pick arm, each by itself: damage a minute before the boss at least 0.95, and damage dealt to the boss a minute at least 0.95 as the median over the runs that reached it, ten reaching it (D-081 item 1). Under either the card costs the run more than taking nothing, and it does not land.
5. Always printed beside a card's figures: its Path's card on the same three measures. That is the hand's real choice. No number is set here for the gap between them; the record on what an evolution must give sets it.
6. The source's second clause, the swarm's time to kill before and after, is printed from the play arms.
7. Said with every reading: the field reads power when the weapon is used. The probe's hero takes no damage and walks inside the crowd, so a contact weapon reads at its best there; whether a build survives using it is the play arms', printed per arm.
8. R-RFG-18's sentence becomes: "Its power is a reading on the build that reaches the hand: on the probe's fixed field the build's damage with the card is `REFORGE.POWER_MIN` to `REFORGE.POWER_MAX` times its damage without it; in play the card costs neither the run before the boss nor the boss fight against a control that declines the hand."
9. The equipment is one branch on `dev/s2` with its own whole gate, built beside D-073 and not inside it: a rule that can be reverted does not carry equipment the exit battery needs. The two fields `strike_hits` and `strike_damage` move into it; this amends D-081 item 6.
10. The equipment keeps itself by its own check, as drafted: with the bot's switch off the five seed 9107 arms and one hold battery read as before, run for run; with it on the run file holds no Reforging pick and the run is the hold's run to the step of that hand; a scene with no pick gives the same window damage to the unit as T-KEE-18's full configuration of the same snapshot; no scene reaches the refill's ceiling; each of the sixteen cards has at least ten builds read.
11. The three landed rules (D-072, D-074, D-079) are read by the band with the other thirteen cards at the exit battery. None is reverted by that reading alone; the record on what an evolution must give decides what an out-of-band card owes.

# D-024 — Every pool row has a door: first-run locks and three Trunk pool nodes

- **Date:** 2026-11-01
- **Decider:** scheduler (delegated, D-009)
- **Class:** RULE
- **Status:** applied

## Context
On a fresh save two Discoveries could not be earned and nine rows could never enter a person's pool. `ScatterKills500` and `RailKills300` count kills with weapons that are `starter=no`, and no Discovery opens those weapons. `Weapon.ScatterCannon`, `Weapon.RailLance` and seven modifiers had no door at all. Six Discovery targets (`S3_discoveries.md`) were `starter=yes`, so their key opened nothing: a Discovery that names them earned a line and no card. D-023 keeps the Tree to pool and utility nodes and names the source's nodes it keeps; it does not name a door for these rows. R-OFF-15 caps Reserve at `C.OFFER.RESERVE_SHARE_MAX` of offered cards and says a pool over it is too thin and is fixed in content; T-OFF-15 reads the tool pool, so the gate cannot see a first run.

## Options
- A — Flip the `starter` column of the six targets and open the nine by more Discovery rows. Every tool run's pool moves, every bot battery and hash goes stale, and the 15 rows are the source's.
- B — Keep the `starter` column as the tool pool. Add a catalog of first-run locks that only a gated pool reads, and three invented Trunk pool nodes for the rows no Discovery names.
- C — Leave the two rows blocked and the nine dark, and write that down.

Inside B, which rows to shut was read on 90 `-FreshPool` runs (`S3_tree.md`): all six fail R-OFF-15 (0 of 81 runs at the boss), the two weapons alone fail (6 of 78), the four modifiers alone pass (79 of 79).

## Decision
B, with four rows. `09_Catalog/first_run_locked.csv` holds the four `starter=yes` modifier targets, shut in a gated pool until `Unlocked` names them. `Weapon.ArcBlade` and `Weapon.RingEmitter` stay open for R-OFF-15, at a cost: `Freeze100` opens no card, and `Survive10` only `Weapon.SeekerSwarm`. `Trunk.ScatterCannon` and `Trunk.RailLance` (`C.TREE.COST_WEAPON`) and `Trunk.Workshop` (`C.TREE.COST_WORKSHOP`, seven modifiers at one price) open the rest. They are pool nodes: R-MET-20 binds them. Tool runs read no part of this (`meta.md` R-MET-23). A first run holds four weapons and four modifiers. `Crit500` and `Elites10` stay unreachable and are listed as such in `meta.md` R-MET-27: changing them is a writer's call on a row, or a new door for Radiance, not this decision's.

## Hypothesis
We believe a person's first run still reads as a game, and that the doors give a run's award something to buy, because a first pool keeps four weapons and four modifiers, Reserve stays under its cap, and the bot's bound holds (69 wins of 90, `S3_tree.md`). Measured by the external S3 session on a fresh profile. Revisited if a first run reads as locked, or two fits on the Orbital Drones and the Arc Blade read as no choice.

# D-043 — DRAFT: S4 Presentation starts on `dev/s4` before the S1, S2 and S3 exit tests, limited to a first wave

- **Date:** 2026-11-10
- **Decider:** scheduler (delegated, D-009). This file is a draft written by a planning agent on `plan/s4-entry`: the scheduler accepts it, changes it or drops it. Nothing in it is in force
- **Class:** GO-NOGO
- **Status:** open (draft, not accepted; `dev/s4` does not exist)

## Context
The owner asked for faster progress and delegates every decision (D-009). S1, S2 and S3 are feature-complete on `main`, `dev/s2` and `dev/s3`. No exit test is passed or waived: all three wait on validators who are not booked (O-008). Almost no automated work is left on those branches. `Validation/S4_artbook_gaps.md` sizes S4 at 212 rows and about 367 session-days and says in its §6 that starting any of it early needs its own D-nnn. This is that record.

What the harness says about entering a stage with earlier exits open:
- H03 §2: a stage's Entry is "previous gate passed". S4's previous gate is the S3 exit, and D-022 says S3 cannot exit before S1 and S2. Read alone, the entry rule says no.
- H03 §3 and `12_Production §2`: the S1 row lists "presentation fidelity" (H03) and "art fidelity" (`12_Production`) under Not before exit. S4 work before the S1 exit crosses that line by name.
- H01: every stage ends with a human exit test and "those sessions are booked before content is". None is booked for any stage.
- H03 §4: "Owner-play checkpoints are gates. The pipeline may queue work past an unplayed checkpoint, but may not merge feel-changing systems past it." This is the one clause that allows work to be built ahead, and it is the ground D-022 stood on.
- H04 §6: presentation changes daily "within the contract", approved by the scheduler, recorded by a golden re-baseline. Art that stays inside `04_Presentation §2` to §5 is the scheduler's layer, not a shape constant or a system rule.
- H08 §2: go/no-go at gates is the owner's. Under a full-auto mandate an unanswered item defaults to the scheduler's recommendation and is recorded as a default.

What D-022 already waived, and what it did not:
- It crossed the same two lines for S3: the Entry rule (S3 started with the S2 gate open) and the S1 Not-before-exit list (the item "meta").
- It waived no exit. S1 and S2 stay open and S3 cannot exit before them.
- It did not name art or presentation fidelity. That item of the S1 list is still barred, and D-022 cannot be read as covering it.

So the harness does not forbid this in a way D-022 did not already cross: the same two lines are crossed on the same ground. Three things are new and are not covered by precedent:
1. A different item of the S1 list (art fidelity, not meta).
2. The stack is one deeper: three exits open under the work, and the S3 exit needs fresh external validators.
3. Meta was invisible in the S1 and S2 exit builds. Presentation is what a validator looks at. If an exit session were run on a build that holds S4 art, its readability and feel readings would no longer be readings of the stage they are for.

What S4-early risks, from the records:
- **The frame.** D-036 moved the camera to 1500 uu on a hypothesis that reverts to 2000 "if any" validator reports a hit from an unseen source. The scale would fall from 36 to 27 px/m, and every detail budget set at 36 is rework.
- **D-036's held items.** Whether screen-wide sim effects follow the frame, the spawn arrival cue (94 % of regular spawns appear in view), and a Warden edge marker are all settled at human readings. Art built on any of them can be thrown away.
- **The palette.** D-008's tokens are the owner's. A reading that moves a ground or hero token moves every material authored against it.
- **Readability rules.** A reading that changes a rule of `04_Presentation §2` (telegraph structure, the dark core, the hull rule of D-010) rebinds the art under it.
- **Contrast margins.** The gate reads telegraph 3.27 and round 3.18 on lit ground against `C.PRE.CONTRAST_MIN` 3.0 (D-036, Implemented). The gap note's §4.3 puts lit ground in a usable window of about Y 0.106 to 0.16: below it the dark band and the round's core fail, above it the tracer core loses its ratio. Any floor, light or weather change spends that margin.
- **Feel.** Hit and kill feedback changes what the player feels. By H03 §4 it may be built and may not be merged past an unplayed owner checkpoint.

## Options
- A — Wait for the exits. Nothing moves until O-008 is booked and three stages are read in turn. The harness's plain reading.
- B — Start all of S4 on `dev/s4`. Fastest, and it spends hundreds of session-days on work that the first human reading can undo (frame, hull rule, floor target, light).
- C — Start S4 on `dev/s4` with a first wave only: rows that need no open human reading, change no sim number and no rule, use no AI-sourced asset, and can be checked without a window. Everything else is held by name.

## Decision
C, as a draft.

1. **Branch.** `dev/s4` is cut from `dev/s3`. `main` stays the S1 build, `dev/s2` S2, `dev/s3` S3. Fixes still flow one way: `main` to `dev/s2` to `dev/s3` to `dev/s4`.
2. **No exit is waived.** S1, S2 and S3 stay open. S4 cannot exit before S3. S4's own exit (contrast in both weathers, perf per tier, humans) is not started by this record.
3. **Nothing merges back.** No commit on `dev/s4` reaches `dev/s3`, `dev/s2` or `main` before the exit of the stage that branch holds. A BUG found on `dev/s4` that exists on an earlier branch is fixed on that branch and carried forward.
4. **Exit sessions are read on their own branch.** The O-008 sessions use the package of `main`; S2 and S3 sessions use theirs. A `dev/s4` build is never the build of an S1, S2 or S3 exit session. The owner may play `dev/s4` at any time; that is a checkpoint, not an exit.
5. **Scope is the first wave**, `Validation/S4_wave1.md`: seven work packages after a foundations package, about 53 of the 212 rows opened, most of them to silhouette grade and not closed. Its four tests for a row are the four limits of Option C. A row outside the wave needs a new record or the exit it waits on.
6. **The sim does not move.** No existing value in `constants.yaml` changes, no rule text changes, and no catalogue row changes. New keys are presentation-only and have no reader in the sim. Proof on every package merge: seed 9107 `input_hash` on the arms the earlier records used, the `Tools/RestartCheck.ps1` hashes, and a fixed bot sample byte-identical.
7. **The contract is not reinterpreted.** `04_Presentation §2` to §5 bind as written. No existing token value changes. The telegraph's three layers, the enemy round's dark core and `Pal.Enemy.Body` on hulls stay.
8. **Assets.** Every asset is generated by a script in this repo, headless: Blender 5.2.2 at the path in `STATE.md`, or editor Python. No AI-sourced mesh, texture or input (O-009's default). `licenses.csv` and its check land before the first binary.
9. **Built to survive a frame move.** Wave 1 art is judged at 36 px/m and must still read at 27 px/m. No wave 1 budget is set below the coarser frame.

Held back, with the reason (the full list is `S4_wave1.md §4`):
- Until the D-036 readings: the Warden's model and size, an edge marker, a spawn arrival cue, every Intercession and screen-wide look, fine hull and hero detail under about 5 px at 27 px/m, conduits.
- Until the contrast margin is re-read on the wave 1 floor: both weather rigs, exposure, fog, cloud, grain, the scalability tiers, standing water, pickups, the low-HP vignette.
- Until a human reading or a call that changes a rule: the ceramic hull (D-010, R-ENM-24), elite parts (the "two elites told apart" check of `elites.md` is human), telegraph chevrons and the polar ring, the Shielded dome, shake and hit-stop, per-weapon hue, damage numbers, the Favor panel, everything `vigil.md` R-VIG-15 bars.
- Until O-009: nothing is waiting on it. The hero is built from scratch either way; the ruling only decides whether RogueHero's `base_*` parts may ever enter.
- Until audio decisions: all audio.

## Hypothesis
We believe starting a first wave on `dev/s4` turns the idle time before O-008 into art that survives the exits, because every wave 1 row is chosen to hold at both frames, on today's tokens and under today's rules; measured at the S1, S2 and S3 exits by counting wave 1 packages reworked because a human reading moved the frame, a token or a readability rule. Reverted, by stopping `dev/s4` and holding what is merged there, if more than two of the seven packages need rework from one reading, or if any package cannot merge without moving a seed 9107 hash.

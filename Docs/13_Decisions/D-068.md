# D-068 — The player's own fire is not drawn within 2.5 hero radii of the hero

- **Date:** 2026-11-12
- **Decider:** scheduler (delegated, D-009; D-057 ruling 10 made the hero's read in a fight wave 1's own acceptance), on the Visual module session's two frames rounds and its reading. The owner's word of 2026-11-11 applies: "ask for the reason, make your decision which helps the game the best"
- **Class:** RULE (presentation: `tracer_art.md` R-TRC-01 and R-TRC-02, on `dev/s4`), with one new constant
- **Status:** applied on `dev/s4` (`s4/hero-clear-zone`, d09d4c9, 2026-11-12); the machine check is met; the owner's sitting is asked as O-017 and the perf reading of record is owed

## Context
In a thick fight the hero is the hardest thing on the screen to find. The figure is pale, about forty pixels tall at 1920x1080, and the player's own rounds are white heads that start on top of it. The scheduler saw it on `combat_staged` on 2026-11-11 and D-057 ruling 10 asked the Visual session for a rework of the figure's read with a machine check.

The Visual session shot held frames at 1920x1080 on a throwaway branch (`tmp/hero-read` c521bdc, cut from `dev/s4` 1bc1d1a, so before package E): two scenes, `combat_staged` and `tracer_crossing` (the worst case: the hero on crossing telegraph bands under its own fire), thirteen variants of three ideas, plain and through the deutan simulation, with measured tables. The scheduler looked at the crops round the hero at three times and at native size, at the lamp crops and at the reduced whole frames.

- Two launches of today's frame give the noise: 0.205 and 0.204 in the zone, 323 and 312 pixels over the figure.
- Why the lamp fails though an enemy optic is the loudest thing in the frame: the optic is twenty pixels and more of a saturated colour on a dark hull; the lamp is ten to thirteen pixels of a pale colour on a pale helm. A lamp that works needs a deep colour, which the palette does not have, and a dark surround, which `hero_art.md` R-HAR-04 gave up to keep armour pixels.

## Options
- A — The clear zone alone, at 2.5 hero radii. The one change that clears the figure by a number, at no measured cost.
- B — The zone with the outline. Clearer still up close, but it breaks R-HAR-06's armour floor, which would have to be rewritten first, and it spends the telegraph edge's tone on the hero.
- C — The lamp, alone or with the zone. It does not show in any colour the palette has.
- D — Nothing until a person has played. The owner's one look would then be spent on a figure we already know is covered by its own fire.

## Decision
A, in eight items.

1. A player round's head and tail are not drawn while the round is within a stated number of hero radii of its hero. First value 2.5, one constant in the `C.TRC` block, named by the job. Draw only: the round flies, hits and counts as before, and the draw side adds no state the sim reads or writes (D-058).
2. It is written as a rule of the hero and not only of rounds: nothing of the player's own is drawn opaque inside the zone. The second wave's weapons (W2-C of D-057) inherit it.
3. The machine check, on `tracer_crossing` (seed 0, held), at both frame sizes: round-head tone covers at most a stated share of the zone from 1.0 to 2.5 radii; round-head tone over the figure's box is at most a stated count; the ring from 2.5 to 4.0 radii still holds at least a stated share, so the check fails if the fire is simply not drawn; with the contrast watch, the sim proof, and a unit test that a round inside the radius has no instance while the pool's count is unchanged. The Visual session proposes 0.06, 20 and 0.05. The limits are fixed from frames shot on `dev/s4` as it stands with package E, because these frames predate E and E's sparks at arm's length may count as round-head tone: the note states the figures on that base first.
4. Perf. Wave 1 has no game thread left to spend: the S4 scene stands 0.11 ms under its ceiling at the worst run and 0.05 of that is audio's (D-056). This job's allowance is none. It reads the S4 perf scene before and after as a bound; if the game thread rises by more than the spread between runs, the scheduler takes the reading alone before the branch lands.
5. The outline is not built now. It is the next step if the sitting says the zone is not enough, from the variant with the rim behind the body, and R-HAR-06's armour floor is rewritten before it, as a decision of its own.
6. The lamp is not built now. It belongs with the hood and the Patron lamp of the second wave, where a deep colour and a dark surround can be designed together. If it is built then, Storm's hue is the one that keeps a distance from the enemy optic under deutan.
7. Granted by name, on `dev/s4` only: the sentences in `tracer_art.md` R-TRC-01 and R-TRC-02, the constant and its row, the test and the check. A golden frame that moves beyond its tolerance because the player's fire near the hero is gone is shot again by this job, and its note lists each one.
8. `tmp/hero-read` is kept, local and unpushed, until the sitting has answered: it holds the outline and the lamp as they were shot, and the outline may be the next step.

## Hypothesis
We believe that not drawing the player's own rounds within 2.5 hero radii lets a player see their own figure whole in the thickest fire, because the rounds are white heads on a pale figure and they are the one thing of the player's own that covers it (323 pixels of them over the figure to 1, with the fire still seen to start beside the hero); measured by the check's three figures at both frame sizes, and at the owner's sitting on `dev/s4` by two questions: can you find your hero at a glance in a fight, and does your fire still look like it comes from you. Reverted, by the constant set to 0, if the sitting says the fire no longer reads as the player's own and neither a smaller radius, a fade across the zone nor a mark where the fire starts mends it. If the sitting says the hero is still lost, the zone stays and item 5 is the next step.

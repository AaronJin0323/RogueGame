# D-030 — A card states what it takes, per weapon at its level, in the numbers the sim deals

- **Date:** 2026-11-01
- **Decider:** scheduler (delegated, D-009)
- **Class:** RULE
- **Status:** applied

## Context
Hammer's Key was the only card in S1 that converts, and no card said what it takes. Its rules row said "become", named no weapon, and read ×1.6 where the sim deals ×1.28 (R-WPN-10). The owner took it on a Rifle at level 1 and lost every round, with Scatter Shell asleep. D-027 showed a silenced socket; it did not show the weapon's own fire. Evidence: `Validation/S1_modifier_truth.md`, Card truth.

## Decision
1. R-OFF-13: a card whose pick converts more of a weapon's rounds says what stays in the air, per weapon and at its level, in the box that R-MOD-08 already draws, above the sockets. The numbers come from `DTOffers::RoundsAfterPick`, which composes copies, writes nothing and draws from no stream.
2. A card states what it takes as well as what it gives (`keepers.md` Text). The Key's rules row reads `{cleave_mult}`, `dmg_mult` × `C.WEAPON.CONVERT_MELEE_DAMAGE_RATIO`. This corrects a display: no number in `constants.yaml` or the catalog moved.
3. Two new rows, `ST_Weapons.Rounds.None` and `.Some`, in `ST_Weapons`, because `keepers.md` fixes `ST_Keepers` to exact keys. At zero the sentence that matters is that nothing flies, so it is its own row.
4. The Trait says dormant modifiers are excepted. "Around you" goes from the card and from `keepers.md`: a cleave is a 140° arc in front.
5. The box's text steps down toward the keyword notes' floor rather than grow the card, truncate or merge rows.
6. The gate's `contrast` step also reads the HUD's dormant line against its panel, on a second staged frame (`Tools/Smoke.ps1 -DormantLine`), where a frame with no line fails. The card box stays a hand reading.
7. `DTOfferStyle::TextMul` is a capture hook on `main`, 1 in Shipping. `dev/s2`'s `DTSettings::TextScale()` wins where both define it. A shot turns `dt.Offer.AutoPick` off and levels up a Rifle the Patron pick already gave.

## Impact
- `input_hash` on seed 9107 is unchanged on five arms; `bot.csv` is byte-identical on all five. A hand costs the sim nothing.
- Invented zh, listed in `S1_modifier_truth.md`, needs a native read.
- Held, not changed: Masterwork does nothing in a real S1 run, and `Mod.Fork` adds nothing under a whole conversion. Each is a RULE or content call for a person.
- Not verified: no human has seen the box or the words, and whether they stop the surprise the owner met takes a person.

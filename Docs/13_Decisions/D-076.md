# D-076 — An elite's parts are placed on the hull the enemy has: a mend of wave 1, pulled out of the held wave

- **Date:** 2026-11-12
- **Decider:** scheduler (delegated, D-009), on the Visual module session's finding and proposal of 2026-11-12
- **Class:** BUG (the draw code does not meet `elites.md` R-ELT-10 since package B of wave 1). It amends D-057: the item W2-G of the held second wave is this mend and leaves the hold
- **Status:** applied on `dev/s4` (7382f70, 2026-11-13)

## Context
- An elite is drawn as its role's hull with extra parts: a light, plates, rear parts. `DTElite::BuildParts` places every part from the hit radius as if the hull were a cube of that half-size: the light one radius above the centre, a plate one radius out to each side, rear parts one radius behind.
- Package B of wave 1 gave the enemies hulls that are not cubes: the Swarm 93 by 85 by 23 uu at a radius of 32, the Rusher 146 by 56 by 40 at a radius of 40. On the flat Swarm the light hangs over the hull; on the narrow Rusher the plates stand clear of it. In the staged frames an Armored Rusher reads as a cluster of dark boxes under a floating light.
- `elites.md` R-ELT-10 puts the light "in the same place as the role's optic". Since package B it sits where a cube's optic would be. No check reads the parts against the new hulls, and wave 1's own note says both.
- The Visual session's plan for the second wave lists this mend as W2-G (an anchor table for each role; the parts keep their shapes). The second wave is held until the owner has sat or session A is read (D-057).
- The owner's sitting (O-017) asks whether an elite can be told from a plain enemy and one kind of elite from another. Today that question has a known answer.

## Options
- **Mend it now, as wave 1's.** The defect was made by wave 1 and leaves a written rule unmet; the mend changes no design.
- **Leave it with the second wave.** The hold was put on new design, so that the owner's look could steer it. This is not new design.
- **Wait for the sitting.** The owner's one look would then be spent, in part, on a thing we already know is wrong.

## Decision
Mend it now, in seven items.

1. W2-G leaves the held wave and is wave 1's mend. Nothing else of the second wave moves; D-057's hold stands for the rest.
2. An anchor table for each role, taken from the hull generator's own table, with a tools test that binds the two so that they cannot drift apart. `BuildParts` places every part from it. The parts keep today's shapes, counts and tokens.
3. Granted by name, the draw half only: `Enemies/DTElite.cpp` and `.h` (`BuildParts`; the affix rules in that file are not touched) and its caller in `Enemies/DTEnemySubsystem.cpp` (`SimRender`) if the anchors are passed in. `Tests/Enemies/DTEliteTests.cpp` only if `BuildParts`' signature changes, and then its four call sites, named in the hand-over. A rule in `enemy_art.md` for the anchors. No text of `elites.md` changes: the mend makes the code meet R-ELT-10 and R-ELT-11 as written.
4. The check that binds it: a geometry test for every role and affix (each part's near face within a stated distance of its hull's surface and not sunk past a stated depth; the light's centre at the role's optic anchor at the elite's draw scale); the existing tests `Dusktracer.Elites.Parts` and `Dusktracer.Pre.EliteLight` unchanged; one staged scene of every role as an elite with every affix, with its golden; on that scene, at both frame sizes, the elite light's lit pixels for each role not under today's; the contrast watch; the sim proof.
5. The sim is not touched: the affix rules stay as they are, and the seed 9107 hashes, the restart and the meta hashes cannot move. The goldens that hold elites are shot again by this job and listed.
6. Perf: the same parts and the same instance writes. A pair on one build, read in turn on the same evening, as a bound.
7. The sitting is not held for it. If it lands before the owner sits, the line on floating elite parts leaves the known gaps of O-017 and question 8 becomes a real question; if the owner sits first, the line stands as written.

## Hypothesis
We believe that placing an elite's parts from its own hull lets a player tell an elite from a plain enemy and one affix from another by the parts, because today the parts hang clear of the flat and the narrow hulls and read as clutter beside the enemy and not as part of it; measured by the geometry test for every role and affix, by the staged scene's lit pixels for each role, and at the owner's sitting by question 8; reverted, the anchors put back to the hit radius, if a part's lit pixels fall under today's on any role, or if the sitting says the elites read worse than before.

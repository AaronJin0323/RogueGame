# D-086 — The boss's side of the Reforging band is read on the probe, against the hull

- **Date:** 2026-11-13
- **Decider:** scheduler (delegated, D-009), on the Mechanics module session's Draft 21 (`Validation/boss_side_one_body_plan.md`, `plan/boss-side-one-body` 98743f2), written from figures already held; no scene with one body had been run
- **Class:** METHOD, with measuring equipment. It amends D-082 item 4 and D-085 item 9 (where the boss's side comes from). No rule of play and no number of the game
- **Status:** accepted; not built. The first step, one try of the fight in a probe scene, is the first thing done when work resumes after the pause of 2026-11-13

## Context
- D-085 judges a card on two sides and takes the boss's side from play: the damage dealt to the boss a minute against the arm that declines the hand.
- The reader's first intervals show what play can say there. At fifteen runs an arm the boss's side is known to about a quarter either way; resampled by seed, as D-082 now judges, it is narrower for most pairs and still far too wide for a band of 1.3 to 1.8 (Shade's card 1.06 to 2.12, Quicksilver's 0.99 to 1.80, Radiance's 0.88 to 1.50, Forge's 0.72 to 1.20; the reader's lines before its hand-over). Play can show that a card holds a floor, and can catch a gross cost. It cannot place a card in the band.
- The crowd's side has no such noise because it is paired: the same snapshot and seed with and without the card.
- The Warden is not a large field body. Three of its own rules make it a different target: its size (340 uu against 32), its statuses (a Freeze refused, Chill capped) and its movement (moved by script, displacements ignored, a standoff from the hero in its first phase).

## Options
- **A.** Keep the boss's side in play and run more batteries.
- **B.** One field body that does not die, in the probe.
- **C.** The hull itself, in its own fight, in a probe scene.

## Decision
C, as drafted.

1. **The scene.** For each Reforging card, the level 16 snapshots that the crowd's side reads, each in a probe scene in which the Warden's fight is started and no field stands; with the card and without it, the same snapshot and seed.
2. **The measure.** Applied damage dealt to the hull in the window, which starts after the boss's calibration so that its health is settled. The lift is the damage with the card over the damage without it, the median over the card's builds.
3. **An interval on every figure of the band, on both sides:** the median resampled by build, ninety in a hundred. A card is under or over a bound when its whole interval is; when the interval holds a bound the reading says "at the lower bound" or "at the upper bound" and the card comes to the scheduler with it. D-085's split and its two sets of builds stay.
4. **The band is D-085 item 9's,** with "the boss's side" now meaning items 1 and 2.
5. **Play keeps what it can decide.** The floor before the boss stays. The boss floor becomes an alarm: a card fails it only when its whole interval is under 0.95. No second battery follows by rule.
6. **What the scene is not,** said with every reading: the probe's hero takes no damage and walks the probe's route, so the scene reads what a build can do to the Warden when it does not have to dodge. Whether a build can stand in the fight is play's: reach, deaths, the alarm.
7. **Its own check, before any card is judged on it.** Two bodies read once side by side, the hull and one field body that does not die: if they rank the cards alike and agree within a tenth the cheaper body would have done, and if not the table says what a small body misses. Each card's lift on the hull lies inside play's interval for it; a card outside means the scene does not read what the fight reads. The scene's damage a minute with no pick is printed beside play's. The count of scenes in which a phase changes inside the window is printed. Every card has at least ten builds.

## Hypothesis
None: a method. It is kept if its check holds; reopened if a card's lift on the hull falls outside play's interval for it, or if the interval by build proves as wide as play's.

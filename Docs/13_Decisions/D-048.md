# D-048 — The S1 exit is read in two sessions

- **Date:** 2026-11-11
- **Decider:** scheduler (delegated, D-009; the owner on 2026-11-11: "make the best decision for the game and fix docs, then tell development agents")
- **Class:** GO-NOGO
- **Status:** applied in `Validation/S1_kit.md`, `12_Production §2` and `10_Tuning.md`; the tool half applied on `main` 2026-11-11 (branch `s1/d047-tools`): `Tools/pressure.py --check` takes the statistic of `10_Tuning.md §2` for rows 4 and 5, and `director.md` R-DIR-18 and T-DIR-18 own swarm kill time. Open: both sessions, the gate decision between them, and a session verdict for row 6, which `Tools/owner_read.py run` prints per run and no tool judges

## Context
`Validation/plans_audit.md` 3.2. The kit's pass rule had five clauses, all to be read at one session.

- Clause 3 (the two pressure bands) could not hold in a game with no recovery (D-046).
- Clause 4 (Keeper share) had three instruments (D-047).
- `C.TTK.SWARM_STEERED_MAX` is a Vision success criterion and no clause read it.
- The kit said "median across runs that reached minute 13"; `Tools/pressure.py` computes a per-run mean over time alive.
- Clauses 3 and 4 need runs that live to minute thirteen and level sixteen. The owner, who designed the game, was down at 563 s on level fifteen. First-time players will give few such runs.
- `Harness/H03` makes the S1 exit the place shape constants lock. `Harness/H07 §4` expects sessions every two or three days, not one finale.

## Options
- A — One session, all five clauses. It cannot pass.
- B — One session without the band clause. Shape constants would lock on numbers nobody read.
- C — Two sessions. A reads the people and calibrates; a gate decision sets the bands; B confirms them. The same three people may return.

## Decision
C.

**Session A** (three validators who are not the owner, two runs each; at least one on a pad, at least one in zh-Hans) judges three rows:
1. the six runs were played;
2. at least two of three name their build in their own words;
3. the owner still wants to play after it.

It reads, with no verdict: damage taken and dashes for every minute of every run, swarm kill time, pause seconds per minute, the level-eight and level-sixteen times, the pickup ratio, restores collected, attribution of any run that reached the check level, whether anything hit from off screen (D-036's revert trigger), and whether an elite felt like a fight or a wall.

**The gate decision** then sets the two bands (D-046), names the bot skill row that brackets the validators, and keeps, changes or removes the chest restore.

**Session B** (the same people may return; two runs each) judges, on runs alive through `C.DIR.BAND_WINDOW_S`:
4. damage taken and dashes per minute land in the bands the gate decision wrote, by the statistic in `10_Tuning.md §2`;
5. swarm kill time is within `C.TTK.SWARM_STEERED_MAX`;
6. hands pause the run for at most `C.OFFER.PAUSED_S_PER_MIN_MAX`.

**The tool row**, read on each session's build hash and required at B:
7. Keeper share and the archetype band by loss (D-047).

S1 exits when rows 1 to 3 have held at either session and rows 4 to 7 hold at B. Then `08_Constants §1` locks. A fail routes its findings through `Harness/H04`; it never adds content.

"Asked for another run unprompted" is recorded at both sessions and reported. It is a Vision criterion and it gates the S3 exit (D-049), as `12_Production §2` has it.

## Hypothesis
We believe two sessions move the S1 exit from a rule that fails whatever is played to one whose every row can be read, because each band is written before the session that judges it; measured at session B; reverted to one session if the gate decision finds A's readings already inside bands it can defend and the owner says lock.

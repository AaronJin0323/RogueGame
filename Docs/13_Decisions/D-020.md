# D-020 — Pulse Wall's `dps` is a ratio of one round's damage, per enemy

- **Date:** 2026-10-31
- **Decider:** scheduler (delegated, D-009)
- **Class:** RULE (wording only; no behaviour, number or code path moves)
- **Status:** applied

## Context
1. R-RFG-12 said an enemy in the band is struck for "`dps` × the composed damage per second". That parses two ways (`S2_evolutions.md`, Number and rule problems). At Ring Emitter level 6 (`Damage` 11, `Count` 24, `Cooldown` 0.96):
   - **A**, (`dps` × composed `Damage`) per second, per enemy: 2 × 11 = 22 per second, 5.5 a tick. The code does this.
   - **B**, `dps` × (the weapon's composed output per second): 2 × 11 × 24 / 0.96 = 550 per second.
2. The Handoff never states a damage for Pulse Wall. `build_depth_spec.md` §1.4 and `build_depth_text.md` give the ring, its 3 s and the push. The design log's step 4 (`02_TechnicalDesign_s17_decision_log_HISTORY.md`, 2026-10-20) adds the 0.6 s growth and the radial knock. So the value of `dps` is ours, `[H]`, and the source cannot judge it.
3. The Handoff does say what a zone's rate is measured in, and the log calls a Pulse Wall volley "one zone":
   - Week 3 composition entry: "Lingering Field 70 uu zone ticking every 0.25 s for 25 % dmg"; zones "hit everything inside once per tick".
   - Forge × Flak Mortar crater: "ticks every 0.5 s for 20 % dmg".
   - `keeper_identity_spec.md` §2: `Field` takes "dps %".
   Each is a percent of one hit's damage, taken by each enemy inside. None uses the weapon's total output, and no source zone is scaled by `Count` or `Cooldown`.
4. This project uses the word the same way. `keepers.md` Param units: `dps` is a ratio of the triggering hit. `Flag.Field` opens with `dps` × the hit's damage (`DTWeaponSim.cpp`). `weapons.md` R-WPN-09 composes `Damage`, `Count` and `Cooldown`; a composed damage per second is not a field of the spec, and DPS appears in the balance tables only as a derived figure.
5. The row's own note, written with the number (`number_change_log.csv`, reforging.csv Evolve.RingEmitter): "the band hurts about what one ring round does per enemy twice over". That is A.
6. The same spec names the count when it wants it. R-RFG-05, R-RFG-08 and R-RFG-10 say "the row's damage times count", and R-RFG-06 "the row's total damage". R-RFG-12 says neither. Those three evolutions fold a whole volley into one strike and keep its total; Pulse Wall folds a volley into one ring and, as written, does not. That contrast is the best argument that the ring is underpowered, but it is a fact about the rule, not about what the rule says.
7. Scale check. Per second in units of one hit: Lingering Field 1, crater 0.4, Pulse Wall under A 2, under B 50, on every enemy in the band at once.

## Decision
Reading A. A ring strikes each enemy in its band for `dps` × the composed `Damage` × `tick_s` each `tick_s`. `Count` and `Cooldown` do not enter it. R-RFG-12 is reworded to say so.

The Handoff settles the unit, not the value. `dps` 2 stays a first guess, and whether a ring should deal damage at all, beyond the push the source names, stays an open design point.

## Result
No BUG: the code already does A, so nothing was fixed and no `-Carry` reading was owed. `Dusktracer.Reforging.PulseWall` now pins the tick so the reading cannot drift. A weak Pulse Wall (`S2_evolutions.md`: 1 win against 10 for its hold, bot bound) is a TUNE of `dps` or a RULE on the band, held for a human reading like the other evolutions. B must not be used as a way to raise it.

## Impact
`reforging.md` R-RFG-12, T-RFG-12; `DTReforgeTests.cpp`; `S2_evolutions.md`.

## 1. Camera

The camera is a fairness contract before it is a look: what can hurt the player is on screen before it can hurt them (`00_Vision §2.1`, `enemies.md` R-ENM-13). Decided in D-036; the raised frame in D-080.

- **R-UX-01** Solo framing: the camera looks down at `C.ARENA.CAMERA_PITCH_DEG` with a horizontal field of view of `C.ARENA.CAMERA_FOV_DEG`, aimed at a floor point `C.ARENA.CAMERA_AIM_SHIFT_UU` from the hero toward the camera, at the distance that puts `C.ARENA.CAMERA_FRAME_RADIUS_UU` in half the frame height at the 16:9 target (`C.BUDGET.RES`); a wider window shows more ground at the sides, never less height. The reach from the hero on the plane bodies are drawn on (`C.SIM.PLANE_Z_UU`) is equal up the screen and down the screen, and each is at least the farthest point an enemy attack can start, the largest of `C.ROLE.TURRET_FIRE_RANGE_UU` plus `C.ROLE.TURRET_RADIUS_UU`, `C.DIR.THREAT_RING_MAX_UU` and `C.BOSS.HAZARD_MIN_RADIUS_UU`, plus a Tank's diameter (`C.ROLE.TANK_RADIUS_UU`): counted down to the bottom edge and up to the lower edge of whatever the HUD draws across the hero's column (the boss bar). The frame is the tightest that meets it at this pitch and field of view. Telegraphs lie on the floor, where the same frame reaches a little less down the screen; the farthest, the Warden's ring at `C.BOSS.HAZARD_MIN_RADIUS_UU`, is inside it. A range or a HUD row that moves past it fails the test: the frame, the range or the HUD changes, never this rule.
  - T-UX-01 `Dusktracer.UX.CameraReach`: from `ADTCameraPawn::ComputeFramingDistance`, the constants and the HUD's own layout rows on the hero's column (read as inputs), the reach on the drawn plane at the target aspect up to the HUD's lower edge and down to the bottom edge is equal within one pixel at the top, and each is at least the farthest attack start plus a Tank's diameter; a frame one step tighter, aimed for an equal reach, misses it; D-036's centred frame, a frame solved without the HUD and one with no body margin fail it; the camera's follow offset and lens are that frame; a wider and a narrower window keep the height in view.
- **R-UX-02** The frame is fixed through a solo run: no zoom with threat, density, the boss or a pause, and no player zoom setting. A shared co-op camera (`coop.md`, not written) may widen it to hold both heroes, never narrower than R-UX-01, as its own decision, and `Tools/Perf.ps1` then measures the widest frame it allows.
  - T-UX-02 `Dusktracer.UX.CameraFixed`: the camera pawn takes no zoom input (it never ticks, takes no input and has no arm; the input set binds no zoom action and no mouse wheel) and its offset from the hero, its view direction and its field of view are the same at run start, in a Threat beat, under a held pause and in the boss fight.

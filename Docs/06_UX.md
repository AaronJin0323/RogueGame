## 1. Camera

The camera is a fairness contract before it is a look: what can hurt the player is on screen before it can hurt them (`00_Vision §2.1`, `enemies.md` R-ENM-13). Decided in D-036.

- **R-UX-01** Solo framing: the camera looks down at `C.ARENA.CAMERA_PITCH_DEG` with a horizontal field of view of `C.ARENA.CAMERA_FOV_DEG`, at the distance that puts `C.ARENA.CAMERA_FRAME_RADIUS_UU` in half the frame height at the 16:9 target (`C.BUDGET.RES`); a wider window shows more ground at the sides, never less height. Its down-screen ground reach from the hero, the shortest in any direction, covers the farthest point an enemy attack can start, the largest of `C.ROLE.TURRET_FIRE_RANGE_UU` plus `C.ROLE.TURRET_RADIUS_UU`, `C.DIR.THREAT_RING_MAX_UU` and `C.BOSS.HAZARD_MIN_RADIUS_UU`, plus a Tank's diameter (`C.ROLE.TANK_RADIUS_UU`). A range that moves past it fails the test: the frame or the range changes, never this rule.
  - T-UX-01 PLANNED(S1) `Dusktracer.UX.CameraReach`: from `ADTCameraPawn::ComputeFramingDistance` and the constants, the down-screen reach at the target aspect is at least the farthest attack start plus a Tank's diameter.
- **R-UX-02** The frame is fixed through a solo run: no zoom with threat, density, the boss or a pause, and no player zoom setting. A shared co-op camera (`coop.md`, not written) may widen it to hold both heroes, never narrower than R-UX-01, as its own decision, and `Tools/Perf.ps1` then measures the widest frame it allows.
  - T-UX-02 PLANNED(S1) `Dusktracer.UX.CameraFixed`: the camera pawn takes no zoom input and its offset from the hero is the same at run start, in a Threat beat and in the boss fight.

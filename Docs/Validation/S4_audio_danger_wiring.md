# S4 audio danger wiring: the Warn and Danger observers, the heartbeat's two forms

Job 5 of `Validation/S4_audio_wave1.md` §8.1 (D-056), its second half. Branch `s4/audio-danger` from e910f6b, with `s4/audio-snd-danger-ok` 42dcd73 merged (the nine rows `delegated 2026-11-12`, playable, their eleven SoundWaves); `dev/s4` was at the same commit. The spec is `02_Systems/audio.md`; the first half is `Validation/S4_audio_danger.md`.

**No run has played a voice aloud.** Every run made for this job was a tool process (the automation tests, the gate, the bot runs, the perf scene): no engine sink was made and the application volume was held at zero. The danger sounds are raised, scheduled and given their assets; nobody has heard them through the game.

## 1. The heartbeat's dip, in numbers

Computed in `Dusktracer.Audio.Heartbeat` and `Dusktracer.Audio.LowHpHold` from the scheduler's own duck curve, sampled once a millisecond over one beat in a steady low stretch (the real table's heartbeat row: a voice of 400 ms; one beat every `C.HUD.LOW_HP_PULSE_S` = 0.8 s; the duck's attack 20 ms, depth `C.AUD.DUCK_DB` = -6 dB, release 250 ms). Not estimated.

| Form | How it plays | Depth | Below full, per beat | Average level over a beat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| a (the default; Shipping) | each beat ducks the Player group like any danger voice | -6.00 dB | 650 ms of every 800 (the 400 ms voice, then the 250 ms release) | -3.86 dB averaged in dB; -3.49 dB averaged as gain |
| b (`dt.Audio.LowHpHoldDb` below zero) | the Player group is held at the set depth for as long as health is low; the beat adds no duck of its own | the set depth (-4.00 dB at -4) | all of it, level: no movement at a beat | the set depth |

So form a pumps: the Player group drops 6 dB at every beat and is back at full for only 150 ms before the next one. Form b sits still.

**Form b is the game's.** The scheduler ruled it on 2026-11-13 at 01:02 (its board; D-056's Result on `main` records it): while health is low the Player group is held 4 dB lower and stays level, the heartbeat on top; a warning at low health still ducks to its full depth. The reason, as ruled: a level that moves five times in four seconds, for minutes, is what the owner's direction rules out, and a steady fight keeps the player's fire readable.

- This branch still plays form a by default and carries the switch (`dt.Audio.LowHpHoldDb`, not in Shipping). Form b becomes the default, with a key for its depth, as the first commit of the fire family's wiring job. Until then no player-side sound exists in the game, so the two forms cannot be told apart in the game today: the duck lowers `SC_Player`, and nothing plays in it yet.
- The figure for b: the mix model reads the heartbeat 6.3 dB over the stand-in mix with no lowering, so 10.3 dB at a hold of -4, against `C.AUD.HEARTBEAT_SMR_MIN_DB` (9). Against the real fire of `s4/audio-snd-fire` d3c9d1e (the Sound session's reading, not this branch) it is 5.5 dB with none and 9.5 at -4.
- Under form b a warning or another danger voice still takes the group to `C.AUD.DUCK_DB` and back to the hold. What only an ear can still judge is the level the fight sits at under the hold; nothing waits on it.

## 6. What is not verified

- **Nothing has been heard**, and nothing waits on it. What an ear would judge first: the warnings against each other in a pack, the fall alone at the end of a run, the fight's level under the low-health hold (§1).
- The heartbeat in a real run: no bot run of this job went low on health, so the observer's reading of the hero's state in a world is proven by the build and the staged tests, not by a run that beat.
- Hurt, down, ring, arc and the boss's phase in a real run (§3).
- Form b at the engine: the held depth reaches the engine as the same Player-group curve, through the same sender; the engine sink exists only in a person's launch.
- Two danger sounds asked in one step start in one frame, and the mix model reads one cue on top. The Sound session read every pair of the nine at their catalog volumes on 2026-11-13, with `Tools/audio/mix_model.py`'s own functions (a session script, not in the repo). Of the thirty-five pairs that can start together (the hurt and the down no longer can), three pass `C.AUD.LIMIT_DBFS` (-1 dBFS) with nothing under them: Band with Ring +0.06, HeroHurt with BossPhase -0.23, Line with Band -0.41. On the densest stand-in mix, ducked, nineteen pass it and six reach full scale or more; the worst is Band with Ring at +0.99 dBFS. Nothing in the engine limits the sum: R-AUD-04 names no limiter. In the game as it stands the player's side is silent, so only the three bare figures can occur. A limiter on the game's own submix, and a reading of pairs in the model, are proposed to the scheduler for the wiring of the first player-side family.
- The match distance (150 uu a step) is read from the enemies' and the boss's movement, not measured over every tell; a telegraph that jumps further in one step would warn twice.

## 7. Not in this job: the last fifth of a tell

A mark in the last fifth of a telegraph needs the lead's length, which is not known at its first step: the view publishes only `Progress`. It could be read from the second step on (the step's growth of `Progress` gives the lead in steps), or from a public getter of the lead's length, which would be an edit to the enemies' and the boss's files.

# S4 art book gaps: the book against `dev/s3`

For whoever plans S4. Compared against `dev/s3` at 5ccf82e and RogueHero at 50a1506. The art book is held by the owner and is not in this repo: two volumes, main (29 boards: PL.00 to PL.24, A1 to A4) and Cast-off (18 boards: PL.C00 to PL.C17). Plates are cited by id only.

This note starts nothing and decides nothing. It records no decision and changes no spec. S4 sits behind the S1, S2 and S3 exit tests (`12_Production §2`, D-022). The book is non-binding reference (`04_Presentation §6`, "this document wins"), so on any clash §2 to §5 of `04_Presentation`, the specs and the D-nnn stand. Where this note says which side stands, it is reading that order, not making a call.

Sizes use RogueHero's gap-list scale, for one session: S up to 0.5 d, M 1 to 2 d, L 3 to 5 d, XL over a week. They are estimates. "The token table" means `04_Presentation §3`; a bare § points inside this note.

## Summary

- **The camera call comes first, before O-008.** The book's camera sits 29 m back and shows about 27 m across at 71 px/m; `dev/s3` sits about 76 m back and shows 71 m across at 27 px/m (§2.8 has the sums). Every detail budget, the void past the plate, the spawn rings and the Warden's size hang on it. No decision records the framing and `06_UX.md`, which `04_Presentation §6` says owns it, does not exist. S1 to S3 human readings are redone after a move, because they are taken at today's frame.
- **The sim is far ahead of the screen.** Every enemy, the Warden included, is the engine cube; the hero is a cylinder; rounds are spheres; gems and chests are cubes. `Content/DT` holds four materials and one map, and no texture, mesh, font or sound. No hit, kill, status or Intercession presentation exists.
- **Effects are mostly a port, hero and world are new work.** RogueHero built most of PL.14 to PL.19 and the UI. But the book's fusions and Intercessions draw RogueHero's design, so only the look carries, and each needs a call on what it shows.
- **The hero cannot simply be ported.** RogueHero's model is AI-service output (licence block, §5) and is not the book's figure. The own-work Blender generators and bake are the part worth taking.
- **The hull rule is unsettled, and the book splits on it.** Cast-off boards draw bone ceramic over dark metal; main-volume game frames, A1 and A4's prompts draw dark hulls (§3.4); R-ENM-24, T-ENM-24, R-ELT-11 and D-010 hold `Pal.Enemy.Body` #1C1E24.
- **The foundations are missing.** No `licenses.csv`, `catalog_lint.py`, `SourceArt/`, Blender tool, presentation spec, sound path or second weather. The Warden is a kitbash of six role models, two of which are S5 roles.
- **The book is not a readability proof.** Its rounds lack the dark core, its telegraphs lack the dark band, and some plates hide the optics and the hero. Follow the contract, not the render (§3).
- **S4's exit has holes no art item fills** (§7): "the reference bar" has no definition, only the 4070 class has a GPU budget key, and one golden re-baseline does not fit art landing in steps.
- **The size.** 212 rows, 211 of them S4 work, about 367 session-days at range midpoints, about 431 with the 15 percent reserve (§6.5), before the items in §7 that have no size. The critical path is human, and no source sizes it.

**Framing sums.** Today's source: `Game/DTCameraPawn.cpp` (`FollowTransform`, `ComputeFramingDistance`, `FrameAspect` 16/9) with `C.ARENA.CAMERA_PITCH_DEG` 60, `C.ARENA.CAMERA_FOV_DEG` 50 (horizontal) and `C.ARENA.CAMERA_FRAME_RADIUS_UU` 2000. The sums take p for pitch, v for half the vertical FOV, 1920 px across, 16:9, and the hero's actor point at frame centre. ## 7. What S4's exit needs that no item covers

S4's row: art to the reference bar, audio signatures, perf tiers. Exit: 3:1 contrast in both weathers, p50 and p95 per tier, goldens re-baselined once. H03 adds contrast and readability sheets.

Without a definition nobody can say an art row is done, and the sizes above are bounded by nothing. Close-up renders are not a usable bar at 27 px/m. The row's origin is `Handoff_Dusktracer/game/G03_Retrospective_EN_ZH.md:287`: environment, hero, enemies, VFX, UI and weather to the art book.

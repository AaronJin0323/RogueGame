# S4 audio: the mix model

> The off-line mix of `02_Systems/audio.md` R-AUD-18 and T-AUD-18 as built on `s4/audio-mix`: `Tools/audio/mix_model.py`, the stand-ins in `Tools/audio/recipes/standin.py`, and the readings' place in the `audio` step. The plan is `S4_audio_wave1.md` §5 item 3 and its density readings; the method was the Sound session's working sketch.

## 1. What it proves, and how

"Danger is heard first", as numbers. The model builds the loudest player mix the scheduler allows and reads every danger sound of the catalog against it.

1. **Roles.** The player side of a fight is five roles: fire (one voice stream per weapon), hit, kill, the crowd layer, the low layer. A catalog row takes a role by family (`Fire`: each row is one weapon) or by id (`Snd.Kills.Hit`, `Snd.Kills.Kill`, `Snd.Kills.LayerCrowd`, `Snd.Kills.LayerLow`). None of these rows exists yet. A role with no row plays its stand-in, at the levels of the model's table, and the output always names which roles were stand-ins.
2. **Tiers.** 5, 20, 60 and 100 kills a second, with 3, 6, 10 and 10 weapons firing. Ten stand-in weapons: rifle x4, heavy x3, light x3, interleaved so the three of the sparsest tier are one of each.
3. **Schedule.** Each weapon asks at its own cadence, as §3 says (until 2026-11-13 each asked `C.AUD.FIRE_DENSE_VOICES_PER_S` voices a second). Kills: one voice each under `C.PRE.DENSITY_LOD_KILLS_PER_S`, then `C.AUD.KILL_DENSE_VOICES_PER_S` a second. Hits land at 20 a second per kill a second (`HITS_PER_KILL`, from T-AUD-07's sizes: thousands of hits at about a hundred kills a second). A stream's requests are counted per sim step at `C.SIM.FIXED_HZ`, with its cadence jittered (owed requests grow by rate x step x (0.4 + 1.2u)) so the mix is not a clock. The requests of one step are one voice, louder by 1 + its aggregation gain x log2(count), capped at `C.AUD.AGGREGATE_MAX`; a request inside its least interval merges, counted. Grid rows start on the next multiple of `C.AUD.GRID_STEPS` steps. Per 1/60 s frame the candidates go loudest first: at most `C.AUD.STARTS_PER_FRAME_MAX` start, the rest wait one frame and are then dropped; a start past `C.AUD.PLAYER_STARTS_PER_S_MAX` in the second up to the frame, or with no free slot of `C.AUD.VOICES_MAX` - `C.AUD.VOICES_DANGER` - `C.AUD.VOICES_UI`, is dropped and counted. A layer that is on holds one slot (`LAYER_SLOTS`: the runtime's layer is one looping voice). Both are model constants, not keys, confirmed by the scheduler: the game reads neither, and if it ever does, each becomes a key with a change-log row. Variants play in turn per stream; a key voice draws one of its written note's five degrees by resampling, and a level within `C.AUD.LEVEL_JITTER_DB`. The crowd layer is on from `C.PRE.DENSITY_LOD_KILLS_PER_S`, the low layer from `C.IMP.DENSITY_FLOOD_KILLS_PER_S`, both at full level. One seed, through `dtsynth.Rng`: two runs give the same samples.
4. **Render.** Mono floats at `C.AUD.SAMPLE_RATE_HZ`, 8 s a tier, read past the first second.
5. **Readings.** Per tier: integrated K-weighted loudness, the share in `C.AUD.DANGER_BAND_HZ` (at most `C.AUD.PLAYER_BAND_SHARE_MAX_DB`), crest (printed for every tier on the one seed; judged against `C.AUD.MIX_CREST_MAX_DB` only as ruled in §2), and the rise from 5 to 100 (at most `C.AUD.DENSITY_RISE_MAX_DB`). Then the danger side against the 100 tier's mix lowered as it stands while the cue sounds (the lowering holds; its attack is left out), every variant, the worst kept:
   - a `warn` zone row: `audio_metrics.audibility` in the danger band over the mix lowered by `C.AUD.DUCK_DB`, at least `C.AUD.DANGER_SMR_MIN_DB`; the figure with no duck is printed beside it (the same reading shifted by the duck: band powers scale with the gain squared);
   - a `low` zone row (the heartbeat): audibility in `C.AUD.LOW_ZONE_HZ` over the mix held `C.AUD.LOWHP_HOLD_DB` lower, its low layer lowered by `C.AUD.LOWHP_LOWEND_DUCK_DB` as well, at least `C.AUD.HEARTBEAT_SMR_MIN_DB`. The heartbeat adds no duck of its own (R-AUD-12, form b, the game's since the fire family's wiring): while it runs the player's side is held at the hold's depth, so that is the mix it must lead. The "no duck" column is the same reading with the hold taken off;
   - a row of family `Warn`: its played loudest 100 ms over the ducked mix's, at least `C.AUD.WARN_OVER_MIX_MIN_DB`;
   - every danger row: the mix lowered as above (ducked; held down under the heartbeat) with it on top, at alignments `C.AUD.MASK_WINDOW_MS` apart, peaks at most at `C.AUD.LIMIT_DBFS`;
   - the control: the loudest player-side one-shot, read as a warning, must not reach `C.AUD.DANGER_SMR_MIN_DB`. If it does, the step fails: the model is broken;
   - the pairs: danger never waits, so two danger sounds asked in one step start in one frame. Every pair of two different danger rows that can (all but `Snd.Danger.HeroHurt` with `Snd.Danger.HeroDown`, `EXCLUSIVE_PAIRS`: a step that holds a down raises the fall alone), each at its loudest file (the variant with the highest peak at its catalog volume) and both on the same sample, is read for its peak bare and on top of the ducked mix at the same alignments. The model prints one line a pair, worst on the mix first, and a count line: how many pass `C.AUD.LIMIT_DBFS` bare and on the mix, and the worst of each. A pair over the line is not an error: the limiter on the game's submix (`SMX_Game`, R-AUD-04) is for it. The mix alone over the line, or one cue on top over it, stays the error it was. The verdict of the step adds the counts, for example `(35 pair(s): 3 bare and 19 on the mix over C.AUD.LIMIT_DBFS, for the limiter)`. The pairs are cached with the other readings.
6. **In the step.** With no danger-side row in the catalog there is nothing to read; the verdict says `not active yet: the mix checks (R-AUD-18)`, as before. With one, the readings join the `audio` step and the verdict names them, for example `mix: 9 cue(s) over stand-ins for fire, kills, layers`. They are cached in `Saved/AudioGen/mix.json` under a key over the recipes and the core (the build's snapshot hash), the catalog, the manifest, the bytes of every file the model reads, the keys it reads, `mix_model.py` and `audio_metrics.py`. The rendered stand-ins are kept as exact doubles in `Saved/AudioGen/standins/`, keyed on `dtsynth.py`, `recipes/world.py`, `recipes/standin.py` and the sample rate.
7. **Audition.** `python -I Tools/audio/mix_model.py --render <dir>` writes 16-bit mono WAV at played level: each tier's mix, and the 100 tier's mix with every danger sound on top in turn, two seconds apart, with the duck's curve on the player side (down within `C.AUD.DUCK_ATTACK_MS`, held while the sound lasts, back over `C.AUD.DUCK_RELEASE_MS`, linear in dB; under a `low` zone cue the same slopes to `C.AUD.LOWHP_HOLD_DB` instead, the deeper of the two winning where they meet) and the low layer's own duck under a `low` zone cue. It plays nothing.

Under it the model prints `crest not judged: stand-ins for fire, kills, layers (printed on one seed)`.

Loudness rise, 5 to 100: 5.1 dB (limit 6). The band share holds by more than 20 dB in every tier. The start cap binds in the two densest tiers (60 starts in a second, 138 to 139 dropped in 8 s), the voice cap never (17 of 22). The control, `standin.fire_heavy` variant 2, reads -66.7 dB as a warning, far under the floor.

**Crest: the ruling (2026-11-12, option A with the median).** The crest is printed for every tier and judged against `C.AUD.MIX_CREST_MAX_DB` only where both hold: no role of the mix is a stand-in, and a layer is on in that tier. A sparse mix is single sounds with silence between, and peak over RMS says nothing there. Where it is judged, the reading is the median over `CREST_SEEDS` = 5 seeds, the run's own seed first: one seed's draw moves the crest by more than 1 dB and the limit sits inside that, so one seed over the line by a tenth is not a finding. The count is odd, so the median is one seed's own reading (an even count would take the mean of the two middle ones). The extra seeds are rendered only when the crest is judged, so today's run with stand-ins costs no more than before. The other readings stay on the one seed. The key is unchanged.

For scale only, since nothing judges it: read as if no role were a stand-in, the stand-in mix's five seeds give 13.9 to 16.9 dB at 20 kills a second (median 14.1), 12.7 to 15.3 at 60 (median 15.0) and 14.1 to 14.8 at 100 (median 14.4). Those extra seeds cost about 2.7 s.

**What follows when the first real family replaces a stand-in.** Its role leaves the stand-in list and the verdict names one role fewer, but the crest stays unjudged until the last stand-in has gone. Every other reading moves with the new family at once: band share, rise, audibility, over-mix, peak and control. When `Fire` and `Kills` both land with their four ids, the crest is judged in the 20, 60 and 100 tiers on the median of five seeds. A median over the key then fails the step, and the fix is in the family's levels or the ruling, not in the seed.

**Test cues.** A ping of E7 and G7 at 0.25 reads 36.3 dB of audibility (30.3 with no duck) and 6.4 dB over the mix; a thump of A1 and A2 at 0.6 read 13.8 dB in the low zone under the duck, so about 11.8 under the hold of -4 dB (to be read again at the fire wiring's gate). These are fixtures of the tests, not sounds of the game. `Mix.test_the_heartbeat_is_read_under_the_hold_not_the_duck` shows on them that the heartbeat's reading is the held mix's and its peak on top too, that a deeper duck moves a warning and not the heartbeat, and that a deeper hold moves the heartbeat and not a warning.

**Time.** The step's added cost with nothing changed is its tests (about 5 s of the step's 9 to 12 s on this PC while other sessions run; 4.8 s before). A recompute on a changed key, with nine cues, takes about 18 s on top, and about 3 s more once the crest is judged.

## 3. The fire of the fullest build; where the crest and the heartbeat are judged (2026-11-13)

Ruled by the scheduler (D-056's Result): the model passed its crest limit only because it fired every weapon `C.AUD.FIRE_DENSE_VOICES_PER_S` times a second, sixty voices of fire that no build makes, and the crest measures how full a fight is, not the sounds. So the model is made truer and the crest is judged where a ceiling on it means something. The figures of §2 are of the model before this; the new ones are read at the fire wiring's gate.

1. **The fire.** The fullest build a run holds (`fire_plan`, the scheduler's ruling of 2026-11-13): `C.WEAPON.SLOTS` = 4 weapons, the four loudest `Fire` rows by played level (each row's loudest variant's `lk100` at its volume), chosen by that rule in code and not kept by name, each at R-AUD-07's cap of `C.AUD.FIRE_DENSE_VOICES_PER_S` a second: on this catalog Rail Lance, Gravity Mine, Scatter Cannon and Flak Mortar, 24 voices a second. The cap is the bound because the catalogs do not bound the fire-rate multiplier: `Transform.Quicksilver` (`reforging.csv`, Slipstream) takes Flag.SpeedScale's `stacks_max` away and leaves a leak (R-RFG-21), so the stacks stand where the hero's speed balances it; with Flag.SpeedScale's `per_stack` (`expression.csv`, Momentum and Rebound), Flag.VolleyOnDash's `tailwind_rate` (Vector) and a Ricochet fallback beside it, any weapon can be driven to the cap. No run holds ten weapons, so all ten together are not a case. A `Fire` row is the weapon of its name (`Snd.Fire.<Name>` is `Weapon.<Name>`; the fire wiring's tool test proves the match both ways); each weapon's own cadence at its last level (one over `cooldown_s`; an Orbiter's count over `cooldown_s`, its drones firing in turn) is printed beside the plan; the ten stand-in weapons take the ten weapons' names in order. The tiers fire the first 3, 4, 4 and 4 of the build; the flood tier, 100 kills a second, is the whole of it.
2. **A row with an aggregation gain** is read at `C.AUD.AGGREGATE_MAX` for its loudness, its peak on top and the pairs; its audibility stays at a count of one. Of the danger rows only enemy fire has one, and the game asks it with the step's rounds as the count.
3. **The crest.** `C.AUD.MIX_CREST_MAX_DB` stays 14. It is judged only on the flood tier, the densest, both beds on, as the median over `CREST_SEEDS`, with no stand-in in the mix. In the other tiers with a bed on it is printed, its median too, and is never an error. A sparse fight's high crest is single strikes in a quiet room, which is what a sparse fight should sound like; what a ceiling on crest can guard is the flood, that it is not strikes over beds too thin to carry them. The model as it stood passed every tier only by firing every weapon six times a second, which no build does, so this loosens a check that was passing for a wrong reason.
4. **The heartbeat** (zone `low`, at the hold of `C.AUD.LOWHP_HOLD_DB`): read over the flood tier's `CREST_SEEDS` seeds, the crest's own (drawn once for both), on two lines (the scheduler's ruling of 2026-11-13, 04:08): the median of the seeds against `C.AUD.HEARTBEAT_SMR_MIN_DB` (9), its own margin, and the least draw against `C.AUD.WARN_OVER_MIX_MIN_DB` (6), the floor a warning must clear. A danger cue is a guard, and a guard is read at its worst case as well as its usual one; on its two worst draws the heartbeat is held to what the game asks of a warning. On this reading the seeds give 9.4, 9.4, 9.4, 7.1 and 7.4 dB: median 9.4 against 9, least 7.1 against 6, and 3.1 with nothing lowered on the least draw. This replaces the earlier rule for form b's depth (the held depth that clears it by 1 dB); judged on the least alone against 9 it failed at 7.1, and the hold stays -4 dB. On the old model it read 9.6 dB on its own draw and 8.2 to 8.5 on the others at the hold: the old margin was the heavy weapons at six a second. The table prints each seed's figure, the median and the least. Tool tests: `test_the_heartbeat_is_judged_on_its_median_and_its_least_seed_of_the_flood`, `test_the_heartbeat_has_two_lines_median_and_least` (passes both; fails the median only; fails the least only).
5. **The rise** is read on the same model, sparsest tier to flood, as before.
6. No level changes for any of this, and the gem is not a role of the model. The cache key covers `weapons.csv`, `expression.csv` and `reforging.csv` (`FIRE_CATALOGS`) and the key `C.WEAPON.SLOTS`.

Tool tests (class `Mix`, test rows as data): `test_the_fire_is_the_fullest_build_of_the_loudest_rows_at_the_cap` (cadences from a fixture weapons table, the cap at any rate, the loudest rows chosen by level, the tiers firing the first weapons of it, a `Fire` row with no weapon named as an error); `test_a_row_with_an_aggregation_gain_is_read_at_the_cap`; `test_the_heartbeat_is_judged_on_its_median_and_its_least_seed_of_the_flood`, `test_the_heartbeat_has_two_lines_median_and_least`; the crest test judges the flood tier alone and prints the others' medians.


## 4. What the model is not

- **Not the engine's mixer.** It sums the files with the scheduler's gains; the engine's own summing, the limiter on `SMX_Game` and the device do not enter: a pair's figure over the line is what reaches the limiter, not what leaves it. The last step, from a voice handed to the engine to air, is the owner's to hear (`S4_audio_wave1.md` §6.3).
- **Band energy is a proxy for hearing.** Third-octave powers with an upward spread of 5 dB per band stand in for masking; "leads in two bands" stands in for "is heard".
- **The stand-ins are drafts nobody has heard.** Their numbers say the method works and where the limits sit; they say nothing about how a fight sounds. Each family that replaces a stand-in goes to the owner by ear first, and the model's readings move with it.
- **A model of the worst case, not of a run.** The rates, the hits per kill and the full-level layers are chosen to load every cap; a real run's voice log (`-DTAudioLog`) is the evidence of what a run does.

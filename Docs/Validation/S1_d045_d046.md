# S1 — D-045 and D-046 applied: what moved on seed 9107

**Verdict: D-045 moves every seed 9107 arm read, each after the first drop-in, and no arm moved without that cause. D-046 moves the no-pick arm, which a restore keeps alive 27 s longer, and leaves the three Storm arms' `input_hash`, outcome and end where D-045 put them although each got HP back from a chest: the hash covers the bot's inputs, the bot reads no HP, and all three win either way. That is a limit of the hash, written down below, not a defect in the restore. The last table's hashes replace the four that every note before 2026-11-11 quotes. Nothing was tuned on these runs (D-050 item 5): four single runs are a record of what moved, not a reading of pressure.**

Build: `da37a86` (before), `ef8bb30` (job 1, D-045), then the commit that applies D-046 (job 2, its parent is `ef8bb30`). Job 1's and job 2's runs were taken on the tree each commit holds, before it was committed, so their run files are stamped `da37a86+dirty` and `355a5d3+dirty` in `run_start.build` (`355a5d3` was job 1's commit before its note was corrected; the code is `ef8bb30`'s).

## Method
`& Tools\BotRuns.ps1 -Arms None,Steered:Storm:Tempest,Steered:Storm:ArcGrid,Steered:Storm:Downpour -FirstSeed 9107 -Seeds 1`, one process at a time, skill 1.0, policy All, the Development editor build of each tree. Outcome, end, steps and `input_hash` are the bot record's (`bot.csv.txt`). HP lost is the sum of `hero_damaged` amounts over max HP for the whole run (`Tools/owner_read.py`'s definition), with the hit count. Rusher hits are `hero_damaged` records whose `role` is Rusher. The first difference is the first JSONL record that differs from the table before, `wall` fields and the build stamp left out.

Why each arm moves, and why that is expected:
- Drop-ins start with the timeline row at 270 s (`09_Catalog/timeline_arena1.csv`), one every 12 s and 70 % of them Rushers. The before runs count 45 (None), 82 (Tempest), 83 (ArcGrid) and 80 (Downpour) drop-ins in their `dir_block` records, so every arm meets Rusher arrivals from about 282 s on.
- An arrival's band is now 1200 to 1400 uu long, not 975, and passes through the hero. The bot steers out of telegraphs (`DTBotBrain`), so its path changes at the first arrival whose longer band reaches it, and `input_hash` covers its inputs.
- No arm differs before 270 s: the first differing record is at 283.8 to 320.0 s on the four arms, after the first drop-in landed. A difference before 270 s would have been a finding; there is none.
- An arm that did not move would also have been a finding, since all four meet arrivals. All four moved.

What these four runs do not say: before the change Tempest and ArcGrid each took one Rusher hit and Downpour none, after it none of the three took one, and Downpour lost more HP. That is one seed per arm on a path that forked at 284 s; it is no reading of D-045's hypothesis, whose measure is `Tools/pressure.py` SOURCES on the nineteen arms. That battery was not run here.

Every chest writes one `hero_healed` record: 4, 6, 6 and 6, of which 2, 5, 5 and 3 restored nothing because the hero was at full health.

Why each arm does or does not move:
- **None moves, as it should.** It opens two chests while hurt, at 495.65 s and 593.30 s, and each gives 15 HP. Its records equal job 1's up to the step the hero went down there (609.25 s), `hero_healed` and `hp_after` apart; with 30 HP more it stands until 636.32 s, takes three more hits and loses 130.7 % of a bar in all. More steps of input are more input, so the hash moves.
- **The three Storm arms do not move, and that is what the hash can see.** Each opens a chest while hurt: Tempest once (9.4 HP at 576.93 s), ArcGrid once (7.5 HP at 565.95 s), Downpour three times (7.8, 15.0 and 2.2 HP at 566.63, 700.95 and 795.70 s). With the `hero_healed` records, `hp_after` and the `wall` fields left out, each arm's JSONL equals job 1's record for record (18288, 12373 and 16001 records), and each CSV is byte-identical but for the build stamp. `input_hash` mixes the bot's move, aim and presses per step (`DTBotSubsystem.cpp`); the bot brain reads no HP; and all three arms win with or without the HP. So a restore that does not change when a run ends changes no input.
- This is the case the job's brief calls a finding, an arm that opens a chest while hurt and does not move, so it is stated plainly: **seed 9107's `input_hash` cannot see the restore on an arm that survives anyway.** The restore itself is read by `Dusktracer.Progression.ChestRestore` and by the `hero_healed` records above. A check that needs the hash to see HP would have to hash state, not input; none exists and none was added.

`Tools/pressure.py` on the job 2 folder prints the new line beside the unchanged damage reading, for example `RESTORES: Steered-Storm-Downpour_s9107 p0 Pickup.Chest 6 for 0.250 of max HP (in window 1, 0.150)`.

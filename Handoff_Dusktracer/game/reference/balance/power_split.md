# Power share by ablation — baseline (measured 2026-10-20, main 065edd4)

Generated by `python -I Tools/Balance/power_split.py`; method in its docstring. Crowd DPS on a refilled swarm field, 10 s windows. A source's share = its drop (full - without it) / the sum of the six drops. Blessings include their Favor tiers and the bullet imbue (they come with the cards). Gain = full / base (start weapon L1, nothing else).

## Findings (balance, for keeper_identity_spec §11)

- **Blessings are 16–19 % of the gain at every snapshot** (L8 16 %, L16 16 %, 20:00 19 %), far below the §4 target of ≥ 60 % at L16. Each patron's own Keeper alone loses 12–69 % of full DPS when removed, but that loss is mostly interaction: removing it also kills what modifiers and the transformation multiply.
- **Modifiers: 1 % at L8, 19 % at L16, 22 % at 20:00.** Bots hold almost no modifiers before L8. The single largest multiplier measured is **Radiance at 20:00: modifiers ×10.7** (31 314 vs 2 921 DPS without them: Crit Lens / Split Core / Fork on a crit build). Next: Radiance L16 modifiers ×5.3, Forge L16 ×3.5, Quicksilver 20:00 ×2.8, Tide 20:00 ×2.7.
- **New weapons are the largest source until L16:** 62 % at L8, 34 % at L16, 22 % at 20:00. The 2nd weapon is not a flat ×2. It ranges from ×0.9 to ×10.9 (Forge 20:00: 544 -> 5 926, the Flak Mortar carrying the build; median about ×2.3). The 3rd weapon ranges from ×1.0 (Radiance) to ×4.9 (Tide L16).
- **Weapon levels: a steady 20–24 %.** **Transformations: 7–8 %** (Storm and Tide about 13–18 %, Forge / Shade / Quicksilver 0–5 %). **Duo reactions: 2–5 %**, with one outlier: Forge 20:00 at 14 % (Storm + Forge, Plasma).
- **One extra pick, next real card of each kind (noisy, see caveats):**
  - +1 modifier: +1 % to +51 %.
  - +1 new weapon: +7 % to +121 %.
  - +1 blessing: −7 % to +14 %, typically +3 %.

  Today a blessing card is the weakest pick of the three.
- **By branch:** almost all blessing power sits in 1–2 branches per run (Storm.Charge 27 %, Radiance.Halo / Blind 27–32 % at L16). Most branches the bots touch read 0–3 %.
- **Build context:** fresh save with no Contracts, so the bots could only take the 18 entry cards. That is the baseline a new player sees; a veteran pool (cores and capstones) is not measured here.

## Caveats
- **Field:** 120 stationary dummies at **10×** the snapshot minute's swarm HP, refilled. At 1× swarm HP the strong builds wipe the field every frame (Storm L16: 49 600 DPS, ~1 270 kills/s). The measurement is then refill-capped and every ablation looks the same, so 10× is the primary table. At 10× the highest rate is 65 kills/s (Radiance 20:00), far from the cap. The cost: kill-chain effects (Overload cascades, on-kill blessings) count less here than in a dense live harvest.
- **Noise:** one 10 s window per configuration. Repeat runs vary about ±10–20 % on the strongest builds, which is why some marginals read negative (Storm L16 +1 modifier −22 %). Treat single cells as rough and the mean rows as the baseline.
- **Shares** are single-removal drops over their sum, so interactions are spread pro rata. A source that only multiplies others (the transformation on Storm) shows a share as large as the product it unlocks.

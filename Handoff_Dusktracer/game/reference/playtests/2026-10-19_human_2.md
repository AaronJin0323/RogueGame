# 2026-10-19 — Human playtest 2

**Build:** `main` at `1603a39`, `Tools\Play.bat /Game/RH/Maps/L_Hub`, KBM. **Tester:** the developer.

## What the tester reported (answers to the then-current playtest request)

1. **Won, and had to dash.** The late-game skill checks and the Warden work as intended for a human.
2. **Cards:** "mostly" readable at a glance.
3. **Level-ups still offer real choices after minute 8.** But **weapons-with-modifiers feel isolated from the Keepers' blessings.** Blessings should buff the weapon's bullets, and each Keeper should have its own feature.
4. **12:00–20:00 was tense.** Enemy bullets hurt the most.
5. No gamepad available (the pad-only check is still owed).
6. **Chinese:** Shade's ultimate "结账" is wrong (a literal translation of "Closing"). Chinese text and names must be written from the whole game's context, not translated from English.

## Decisions (scheduler, on the designer's direction)

- **(3) Design amendment, "blessings imbue bullets":** the Keepers' blessings visibly and mechanically imbue every weapon's bullets with that Path's feature. Storm bullets arc, Forge bullets carry embers and Burn, Tide bullets chill, Shade bullets Mark, Radiance bullets glint and crit-flash, Quicksilver bullets ricochet and streak. A weapon looks and behaves differently depending on whose blessings you hold. This amends GDD §5.4, which today gets elements onto hits only through the Path tiers. Owner: the weapons session, together with the blessing/effect code.
- **(6) Context-first Chinese:** all Chinese names and text are authored from the game's fiction (Narrative doc), not translated. This covers ultimates, blessings, weapons, modifiers, enemies, fusions, places and UI terms. The design docs' 中文 names get updated wherever the new name is better, with the designer's 结账 note as the first case. Owner: the localization session.
- **(4)** No change: enemy fire is meant to be the main threat (GDD §3.5). It stays readable (warm with a dark core).

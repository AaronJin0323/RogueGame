| # | Question | Answer | Read |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Did bullets visibly change after Quicksilver/Shade blessings? | 几乎没有 (almost not) | Matches the log: imbue FX ~0, imbue audio 0. **Bug / visibility failure.** |
| 2 | Warden Form II fair? Different from Form I? | 还不错，但没感受到不同 (fine, but did not feel different) | Form identity does not read. Bot data agrees: II/III no harder than I. |
| 3 | One weapon until minute 13? | 故意没选 (deliberate) | Not an offer bug. |
| 4 | 1 000+ own bullets: exciting or unreadable? | 很难看清敌人的攻击，但是很爽 (hard to see enemy attacks, but very 爽) | **Readability failure (Art §3, non-negotiable 11).** Keep the feeling, fix the contrast. |
| 5 | Noticed Muscle Memory toasts / armour wear? | 没有 (no) | Wear hook not yet on main; toasts fired only at run end, stacked. |
| 6a | Other | 在特定的位置站定敌人的子弹不会打中我 (standing still at a certain spot, enemy bullets don't hit me) | Follow-up: it is **even-numbered aimed spreads** — no bullet lands on the player, so standing still is safe. Pattern bug, not collision. |
| 6b | Other | 中文文本很难懂 (Chinese text is hard to understand) | Follow-up: **all** of it. Designer decision: rewrite every descriptive string in plain Chinese; proper nouns unchanged, no glosses. |
| 6c | Other | 过程中有时会感到无聊，敌人几乎不能近身 (sometimes boring; enemies can almost never get close) | Pressure too low mid-run (matches dmg taken 0 % and 0 dashes/min for long stretches). Violates §3.5 "爽 with teeth". |

## Diagnosis

- **(1) Language freeze:** one click fired 8 culture switches in one frame, each with a font-cache rebuild. Settings-from-Pause also couldn't close (stacked-menu Back bug). The relaunch hangs came from 7 session test games starving the machine. **Fixed:** shared-UI `4a01fa3` + `fb12290` are on main (`f0e455d`); session game launches are now paused during playtests.
- **(3) Level speed:** the XP curve was ~3× too fast. **Fixed:** balance R1 (`XPTable 15/42/115`, growth 1.11; model says L8 ≈ 5:35, L16 ≈ 12:30) is on main. Needs a confirming bot run.
- **(4) Boredom after 15:00** has four causes:
  - The build is finished by minute 6 (XP).
  - The late timeline doesn't ramp: damage taken and dashes are 3–5× under target, so there is no "teeth" in §3.5.
  - Player bullet density is 30–40× under target, so the screen never gets the 爽 look. Weapon R2/R4/R5 (denser streams) is now on main.
  - At full cap the threat roles were dropped. R9 is now on main.
- **(2) Card readability:** this is a new defect. The level-up cards have no per-kind visual language and too little contrast.

## Follow-ups (owners)

- **Enemies session:** late-game pressure. The 15:00–20:00 Threat blocks, elite waves and events must reach the min-15–18 targets (damage taken 30–50 %/min, dashes 6–10/min), with skill-check threats rather than HP walls.
